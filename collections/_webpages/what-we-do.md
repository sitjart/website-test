## Build and foster communities of practice

We run, co-lead and participate in communities of practice in the UK, the wider ELIXIR Europe and beyond.

![](https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/banner-training-keyboard.webp)

## Upskill the life sciences community

Through open and reusable materials, online and in-person events, train-the-trainer courses and tools to discover and index materials and events, upskilling lies at the core of our mission.

![](https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/2023-13-01-fellows-retreat-photo-08.webp)

## Professionalise data stewardship

We run a Fellowship programme of ambassadors across the UK embedding, delivering and creating ELIXIR-UK training and fostering a network of data stewards.

![](https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/finger-touch-on-the-graph.webp)

## Endorse open-access services

We support services for the life sciences community, amplifying their reach and improving their opportunities for long-term sustainability.

![](https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/banner-handshake.webp)

## Participate, lead and develop new projects

Our members lead and participate in national projects, ELIXIR-funded ones and European projects with partners across Europe and beyond.

![](https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/banner-workflow.webp)

## Advancing FAIR Workflows

We enhance reproducibility and accessibility by supporting resources like Galaxy, RO-Crate and Bioschemas, alongside FAIR-compliant standards and WorkflowHub for sharing workflows.

![Data centre with multiple rows](https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/data-center-with-multiple-rows-of-fully-operationa-2023-11-27-05-01-58-utc.webp)

## Supporting Research Data Management

We improve the management, sharing, and preserving of data that’s FAIR and funder-ready. From DMP templates and real examples to UKRI guidance and European collaboration
