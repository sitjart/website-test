## Benefits of becoming an ELIXIR-UK service

- Influence the development of a major effort in data integration: standards, implementations, tools
- Exposure to a wide range of potential users and collaborators across Europe and globally
- Although Node Service status does not confer any additional funding, it does indicate the value placed on the service by ELIXIR, which may be useful for future funding
- Those offering Node Services are eligible to apply for Hub funding for implementation studies and can also apply for Node funding for participation in ELIXIR
- The most significant benefit is the raised profile throughout ELIXIR, its communities and platforms.

## Selection criteria and process

The selection panel assesses services based on:

- Alignment with the five ELIXIR infrastructure themes (data, tools, compute, interoperability, training)
- Impact
- Level of usage
- Sustainability

The process for selecting ELIXIR-UK Services, including selection criteria, is open and transparent, published, and peer-reviewed.

Hancock JM, Game A, Ponting CP and Goble CA. An open and transparent process to select ELIXIR Node Services as implemented by ELIXIR-UK [version 2; peer review: 2 approved, 1 approved with reservations]. *F1000Research* 2017, **5**(ELIXIR):2894 (<https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.10473.2>)

## Prerequisites

- Please note that for a Service to be considered, it must be associated with an ELIXIR-UK Member Organisation. [See full list of Members](/members/)
- All Services are associated with one or more of the [ELIXIR Platforms](https://elixir-europe.org/platforms) (Training, Compute, Data, Interoperability, Tools) and typically serve one or more of the [ELIXIR Communities](https://elixir-europe.org/communities).
