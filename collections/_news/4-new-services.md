Following the 2022 call for new services, we are pleased to announce four new ELIXIR-UK services. These were selected by our panel to ensure high service quality and match UK priorities as identified by its funders.

The successful services were: [RDMkit](https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org) [University of Manchester], [Cafe Variome](https://www.cafevariome.org/) [University of Leicester], [Missense 3D](http://missense3d.bc.ic.ac.uk/) [Imperial College London] and [Variant Validator](https://www.variantvalidator.org/) [ University of Manchester].

We received several high-quality applications and are proud to be representing the best of the UK life science data resources. The successful services will now benefit from their status as ELIXIR-UK services and will be able to apply for funding from the ELIXIR Hub and beyond.

We have also roadmapped three services, [BRepertoire](https://mabra.biomed.kcl.ac.uk/BRepertoire_6/) [Kings College London], [Cyverse-UK](https://cyverseuk.org/) [Earlham Institute] and [Health Informatics Centre (HIC)](https://www.dundee.ac.uk/hic) [University of Dundee] which are well on their way to being infrastructure-ready.
