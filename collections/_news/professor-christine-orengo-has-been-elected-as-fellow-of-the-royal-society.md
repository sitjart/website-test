ELIXIR-UK is proud to announce that Professor Christine Orengo, who has been a member of ELIXIR-UK since its foundation, has been elected as a Fellow of the Royal Society.

Professor Orengo, from University College London, sits on the ELIXIR-UK Steering Committee and is PI of CATH and co-founder of 3DBioInfo.   CATH/Gene3D is a Core Data Resource within ELIXIR.

[Read the full news on the Royal Society site](https://royalsociety.org/news/2019/04/royal-society-announces-2019-fellows/)
