The [University of Manchester](https://www.manchester.ac.uk/) and [the Software Sustainability Institute (SSI)](https://software.ac.uk/) are piloting a new [course on intermediate research software skills for Earth, atmospheric and oceanic sciences](https://uomresearchit.github.io/python-intermediate-development-nerc/index.html) at a [workshop from 27 to 31 March 2023](https://uk-carpentries.github.io/2023-03-27-manchester-intermediate-swc/) at the University of Manchester.

The course is based on the SSI's [Intermediate Research Software Skill in Python course](https://carpentries-incubator.github.io/python-intermediate-development/) from the [Carpentries Lesson Incubator](https://github.com/carpentries-incubator/python-intermediate-development).

The target audience is people who:

- Write code in research environments
- Have gained basic software development skills
- Are seeing their software projects now becoming larger and involving more researchers and other stakeholders
- Are noticing the software is becoming more complex and there are more users and an increasing need to add new functionality
- Now need more intermediate software engineering techniques and tools to develop manage and release their software for reuse by others

Luckily, this new course will cover all of the above!

The workshop will consist of instructor-led code-along sessions, individual self-learning sessions aided by helpers, and small-group exercises mimicking real-life collaborative software projects. At the workshop, they will be joined by colleagues from the University of Bradford and Earlham Institute who are looking to deliver the [original course](https://carpentries-incubator.github.io/python-intermediate-development/) for [ELIXIR-UK](/) and [ELIXIR](https://elixir-europe.org/) audiences.

Find out more [about the course](https://research-it.manchester.ac.uk/news/2022/11/30/reproducible-environmental-research-from-research-it/).
