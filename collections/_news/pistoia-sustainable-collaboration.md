On 17 July 2025, ELIXIR-UK joined leaders from EMBL-EBI, the Pistoia Alliance, and the wider ELIXIR for a high-level strategy meeting held at the EMBL-EBI campus in Hinxton, UK.

The meeting explored new models for sustainable collaboration between commercial R&D, public research infrastructures, and supply-side organisations. Carole Goble, ELIXIR-UK Head of Node, played a leading role in the agenda and discussions throughout the day. She led a breakout session on i**nteroperability and cross-industry collaboration** and chaired the final panel session on **building a sustainable collaboration framework that included funding requirements**.

On this event, the UK Node was happy to attend and participate in discussions alongside our colleagues at **BioFAIR**, the BioCommons infrastructure for UK life science researchers. **Tony Burdett**, Director at BioFAIR, contributed to the panel discussion on sustainable collaboration.

This engagement forms part of ELIXIR-UK’s broader mission to ensure UK contributions to **FAIR life sciences research infrastructures** are well-represented in international conversations – particularly at the intersection of policy, technology and long-term impact and sustainability.

For more on the event, [visit Pistoia Alliance’s website](https://www.pistoiaalliance.org/eventdetails/pistoia-alliance-embl-ebi-and-elixir/).
