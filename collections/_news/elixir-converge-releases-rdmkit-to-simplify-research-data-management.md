ELIXIR CONVERGE has released one of its main outputs, the RDMKit. This project has been co-led by ELIXIR-UK alongside ELIXIR-BE and ELIXIR-NO.

This new tool for empowering researchers in their data management has been a large undertaking and is an important resource which will cater to researchers' individual needs in a wide range of life science disciplines.

The RDMKit is a community-driven project and welcomes contributions, especially those which expand the disciplines covered. [For more information, visit the webpage on how to contribute.](https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/how_to_contribute.html)

[Read more about the RDMKit on the ELIXIR Europe website](https://elixir-europe.org/news/elixir-converge-rdmkit)
