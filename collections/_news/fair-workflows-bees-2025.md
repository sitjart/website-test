On June 30 2025, Professor [Carole Goble](/people/carole-goble/), Head of Node of ELIXIR-UK, was invited to deliver a keynote at the [LifeWatch Biodiversity and Ecosystem eScience (BEeS) Conference 2025](https://www.lifewatch.eu/bees-2025/), hosted by the Hellenic Centre for Marine Research in Crete.

Her talk, *"Sharing and re-using computational workflows – WorkflowHub and FAIR Workflows in Biodiversity"*, explored the role of open, interoperable workflows in supporting biodiversity research and environmental monitoring. She highlighted how platforms like [WorkflowHub](https://workflowhub.eu) and the FAIR Workflows are helping to make complex computational analyses reusable and aligned with the FAIR principles.

The [BEeS Conference](https://www.lifewatch.eu/bees-2025/) brings together European Research Infrastructures, scientists and policymakers to tackle the Triple Planetary Crisis – climate change, biodiversity loss and pollution – through data-driven innovation. The 2025 edition welcomed 193 participants and offered over 50 presentations, featuring hands-on training, poster sessions and cross-disciplinary collaboration focused on ecological responses, virtual labs and sustainability solutions.

Carole’s keynote underlined the importance of infrastructure-level coordination and community-driven tooling to ensure that biodiversity science benefits from robust, shareable and reproducible computational methods.
