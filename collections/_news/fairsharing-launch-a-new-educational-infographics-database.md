[FAIRsharing](https://fairsharing.org/) is a manually curated, informative resource on data and metadata standards, inter-related databases and data policies. FAIRsharing is an [ELIXIR Recommended Interoperability Resource](https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/interoperability/rirs) and an intrinsic registry of the EOSC and the ELIXIR RDM know-how ecosystem.

They have now launched their [FAIRsharing Educational](https://fairsharing.org/educational) component, this fantastic resource is a set of infographics and factsheets covering FAIRsharing content and functionalities for all users and stakeholders across all disciplines.

This will be updated as an ongoing process, but the first six educational materials created by the FAIRsharing team and the [FAIRsharing Community Champions](https://fairsharing.org/community_champions) are now available.

They are:

1. [FAIRsharing in a nutshell](https://fairsharing.org/educational#nutshell)
2. [FAIRsharing content: standards overview](https://fairsharing.org/educational#standards)
3. [FAIRsharing content: databases overview](https://fairsharing.org/educational#databases)
4. [FAIRsharing for you: researchers](https://fairsharing.org/educational#researchers)
5. [FAIRsharing for you: funders](https://fairsharing.org/educational#funders)
6. [FAIRsharing for you: journal publishers](https://fairsharing.org/educational#publishers)

We are excited to see all the new content coming out of this essential initiative!
