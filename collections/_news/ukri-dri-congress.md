On March 6th and 7th, members of ELIXIR-UK (Carole Goble, University of Manchester, Nicola Soranzo, Earlham Institute and Gos Micklem, University of Cambridge) attended the [UKRI](https://www.ukri.org/) Digital Research Infrastructure (DRI) Community Congress. This year marked 5 years since the work was undertaken in the [UKRI Infrastructure Roadmap](https://www.ukri.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UKRI-201020-UKinfrastructure-opportunities-to-grow-our-capacity-FINAL.pdf).

Invited to this meeting were a wide range of participants, including representatives from government, industry, and across the breadth of the research and innovation DRI community.

The content of this two-day event covered the five themes or the DRI strategy. These are:

- Data infrastructure
- Large-scale compute
- Secure services and tools for sensitive data
- Skills and career pathways
- Foundational tools, techniques and practices

We presented a poster for [BioFAIR](https://elixiruknode.org/project/biofair/) during the two-day Congress, and Carole delivered a plenary reflection response.

We look forward to attending any further Community Congress events!
