    ## About the ELIXIR-UK All Hands meeting

    The ELIXIR-UK All Hands is the UK Node's flagship community event, held annually to bring together members, collaborators, and partners from across the UK's life sciences research infrastructure. It is open to all members and collaborators of the ELIXIR-UK Node, spanning research data management, training, human data, bioinformatics tools, digital research infrastructures and much more.

    Previous meetings have taken place in [Exeter (2025)](/event/elixir-uk-all-hands-2025/) and have featured sessions on FAIR data, open science policy, flexible funding calls, AI in the life sciences, and community-led world cafés.

    ### What to expect

    While the full programme is still in development, past All Hands meetings have included:

    - **Keynote and community sessions** from ELIXIR-UK leadership, host institutions and member organisations
    - **Funding and policy updates**, including BBSRC, MRC and BioFAIR updates
    - **Flash talks and poster presentations** from members and early-career researchers
    - **World café discussions** to shape ELIXIR-UK priorities on topics like FAIR data, AI strategy, sensitive human data and training
    - **Networking and social programme,** including dinner on the night of the first day.

    We especially welcome contributions from early-career researchers and those new to the ELIXIR-UK community (including prospective members).

    ### Who should attend

    - Staff and researchers at ELIXIR-UK member organisations
    - Bioinformaticians, data stewards and research software engineers in member organisations (including prospective ones) or collaborators.
    - Policy, communications and project management professionals in research infrastructure.
    - ELIXIR Europe international partners and invited collaborators.
    - Early-career researchers interested in research data management, bioinformatics and open science.
