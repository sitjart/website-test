On 28 May, we're rolling up our sleeves to draft the first FAIR-in-action playbook: How to approach FAIRification.

This is the practical core of the RDM Club's BioFAIR pathfinder project – turning guidance from RDMkit, the FAIR Cookbook, FAIRsharing, WorkflowHub and Galaxy into ready-to-deliver workshop activities, templates, slide decks and facilitator scripts that RDM professionals can take to their own institutions.

[Find out more about the project](/project/fair-in-action-bridge/)

The day is structured into focused sections (full schedule shared soon) so contributors can drop into the parts that match their interests and expertise – for example, FAIRification of specific data types (e.g., imaging, NGS), template design, facilitator scripts, or activity instructions.

You don't need to commit to the whole day.

### What you'll do

- Draft activities, templates and scripts in small breakout groups with acknowledgement of contributions in an open repository.
- Map content to existing FAIR resources
- Test ideas against your own institutional context
- Help shape Playbook v1, which will then be tested across six UK RPOs

### Main audience

Data stewards, data managers, RDM leads, trainers, research technical professionals
