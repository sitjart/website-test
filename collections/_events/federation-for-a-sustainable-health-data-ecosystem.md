The HDR UK Federated Analytics programme is working with existing platform providers, TREs, and the public to drive the adoption of standards, build trust, enable federation, and accelerate research.

In this UK Human Data & Tech Club community call, the team will highlight the role federation can play in sustaining health data research, demonstrate federated querying using [Five Safes TES](https://docs.federated-analytics.ac.uk/five_safes_tes), and identify ongoing challenges and next steps.

### Speaker

**Jonaa Eva**
Project and Outreach Senior Manager
*University of Nottingham / HDR UK*

### What to expect

A 30-20-minute presentation followed by a 10-minute Q&A. This session will be recorded.

### Who should join

This session is particularly relevant for:

- People building tools, platforms, frameworks, or infrastructure to enable federation
- People operating TREs, data platforms, or secure research infrastructures
- People coordinating governance, policy, and standards for federation
- People funding, supporting, or commissioning federated research
- People conducting research that relies on cross-TRE data access
