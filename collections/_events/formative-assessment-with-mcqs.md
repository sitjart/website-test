This month’s Training Club call will focus on **formative assessment and the use of multiple-choice questions (MCQs) in digital training materials** with [Matthew Gemmell](/people/matthew-gemmell/) (University of Liverpool). We’ll explore how MCQs can be designed and applied to support learning.

#### **Main session**

- **Presentation and discussion**: formative assessment and the use of MCQs in digital materials

#### **Community updates**

- Preparing for the ELIXIR-UK All Hands meeting
- Training swaps and opportunities for collaboration
- Onboarding new trainers into the network
- Annual report on ELIXIR Training activities
- Planning possible future training events
- Call for volunteers to lead upcoming sessions

#### **Who should attend?**

- ELIXIR-UK trainers and training coordinators
- Anyone interested in assessment design or developing digital training materials
- Community members keen to contribute to future training activities
