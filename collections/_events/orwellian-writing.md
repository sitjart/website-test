This Training Club session explores practical writing techniques for creating clear, effective teaching materials. The session draws on guidance from George Orwell’s essay *Politics and the English Language*, adapting his principles for use in educational and training contexts.

Through a short presentation and interactive activities, we will translate Orwell’s rules into practical strategies for teaching-focused writing.

The session will cover:

- The use of metaphors – when they help, when they hinder, and how to create new ones
- The benefits and limits of simple language in teaching materials
- Writing with clarity while being kind to yourself as an author
