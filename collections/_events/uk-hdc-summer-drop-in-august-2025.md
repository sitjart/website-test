The August Call of the UK Human Data Community will take place as an informal drop-in during the summer holiday period. With many colleagues away, we’re keeping it light and open-ended – no speaker this month.

Instead, join us for a relaxed session with the community co-leads, Saskia and Tim, to:

- Catch up on current community activities
- Ask questions or raise discussion points
- Share ideas and feedback about our UK Community or the wider ELIXIR human data landscape

Whether you're actively involved or simply curious, we welcome you to drop in and say hello.
