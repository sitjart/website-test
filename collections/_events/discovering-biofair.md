[**BioFAIR**](https://biofair.uk/) **is a proposed new UKRI investment of £34M** to develop a federated digital research infrastructure (RI) connecting existing life sciences RIs, which will be assembled mainly through integrating existing services, resources and knowledge from across the UK.

With the project set to commence in the summer of 2024, we invite you to join the RDM Club with [Gerry Reilly](/people/gerry-reilly/), Interim Director of BioFAIR.

The four core capabilities will be a data commons, method commons, knowledge centre, and community centre with pathfinder projects to validate the capabilities. **This infrastructure will support the broad life sciences research community across the UK.**
