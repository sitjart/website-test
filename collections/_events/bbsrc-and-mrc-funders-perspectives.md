The UK [Human Data Community](/activities/uk-human-data-community/) welcomes BBSRC and MRC funders to their regular meeting to discuss their perspective on funding opportunities for the work on human data.

They welcome **Richard Brown**, BBSRC Senior Portfolio Manager for Data-Intensive Bioscience, and **Michael Ball**, MRC Head of Data Science Theme.
