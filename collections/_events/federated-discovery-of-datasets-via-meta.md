In this month's RDM Club meeting, we welcome Prof [Anthony Brookes](/people/anthony-brookes/) from the [University of Leicester](/members/university-of-leicester/) and Associate Director of the Midlands Substantive Site for the Health Data Research UK Institute ([HDR UK](/members/hdr-uk/)). He will be presenting federated discovery of datasets via meta.

Prof Anthony Brookes’ research and development activities are focused on human genome variation and disease with a core interest in knowledge management to bridge research and healthcare via data science tools and methods. Recent outputs include the GA4GH/IRDiRC standard ADA-M and Beacon, the [GWAS Central database](/service/gwas-central/) and the [Café Variome](/service/cafe-variome/) discovery platform.
