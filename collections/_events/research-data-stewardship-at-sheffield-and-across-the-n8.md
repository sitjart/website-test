The University of Sheffield is strengthening data stewardship across its research community through coordinated initiatives that improve visibility, recognition, and support for data professionals. Current activities include a Library-based [FAIR data stewardship service pilot](https://osf.io/preprints/metaarxiv/2ev6k_v1), mapping stewardship practices across departments, and developing evidence to support a sustainable, university-wide approach.

These efforts have led to the creation of the [Sheffield Data Stewards Network](https://sheffield.ac.uk/library/research-data-management/data-steward-network) and the establishment of a new [N8 Data Stewards Network](https://n8cir.org.uk/our-networks/data-stewards-network/), connecting regional institutions and contributing to the [Research Data Alliance’s Data Steward Career Tracks working group](https://www.rd-alliance.org/groups/data-steward-career-tracks-wg/activity/).

This session will share lessons learned, challenges encountered and future directions for data stewardship at Sheffield and across the [N8 Research Partnership](https://www.n8research.org.uk/).

---

### Why attend?

- Learn how Sheffield is building capacity and recognition for data stewards within the university and beyond
- Hear lessons from running a FAIR data stewardship service pilot and mapping stewardship activities institution-wide
- Discover how the N8 universities are collaborating to strengthen the research data ecosystem

---

### Who should attend?

Researchers, data stewards (formal or informal), research software engineers (RSEs), data managers, institutional leaders and anyone interested in research data careers, professionalisation and FAIR data practices.
