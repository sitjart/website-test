At the July 2025 RDM Club Call, we will showcase work from the [ELIXIR-UK DaSH Fellowship](/activities/fellowship/) and [BioFAIR](https://biofair.uk/), which brings together user stories and RDM tools to support FAIR data management. This collaborative effort, driven by data stewards and research software engineers (RSEs) from 10 UK research-performing organisations, features 17 example data management plans (DMPs) and a collection of guidance videos covering a wide range of Life Sciences data types.

We will also share user stories highlighting the use of the [Data Stewardship Wizard](https://ds-wizard.org/) (DSW), a DMP management tool officially recommended by the [Horizon Europe Programme Guide](https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/docs/2021-2027/horizon/guidance/programme-guide_horizon_en.pdf). These stories demonstrate how the tool can be locally hosted and tailored to suit an organisation’s data management infrastructure.

This session will be a brief signposting talk, with 30 minutes set aside for open Q&A with the authors.
