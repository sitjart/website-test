ELIXIR Europe is due to reopen its [staff exchanges](https://elixir-europe.org/internal-projects/commissioned-services/capacity-building-knowledge-exchange) to support skills development, collaboration and knowledge transfer across Nodes. This Training Club session will bring the UK community together to shape a proposal that reflects our training needs and areas of expertise.
We will work through the following:

- Identify gaps and priorities in UK training capability
- Shortlist the top three topics that would benefit most from a staff exchange
- Discuss where incoming expertise from other Nodes would be valuable, and what expertise the UK could offer in return
- Discuss which communities/platforms/focus groups would align with our proposal
- Draft the outline of a UK proposal to submit to ELIXIR Europe

### **Why this matters**

Attending this session gives you the chance to directly influence the support the UK could receive. A successful proposal may create opportunities for travel, knowledge exchange and targeted skill development that address real needs identified by our community.

### **Who should attend**

Anyone involved in training design, delivery or coordination across ELIXIR-UK.
