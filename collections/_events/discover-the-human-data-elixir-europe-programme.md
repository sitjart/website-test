We are excited to invite you to the upcoming UK Human Data Community Call. This session is a great opportunity to learn more about the ELIXIR Human Data Communities at the European level, their activities and how you can get involved.

Our featured speaker, **Magda Chegkazi**, **Programme Manager for Human Genomics and Translational Data**, will lead a20-minute presentation exploring the work of the ELIXIR Human Data Communities. **Magda** will share insights into current activities, highlight opportunities for collaboration and provide practical guidance for those looking to join or engage with these communities.

Whether you’re new to ELIXIR or already familiar, this session will demystify the role of ELIXIR communities and outline clear pathways for participation. Following **Magda**'s presentation, the second half of the meeting will open up for **questions, discussion and updates** – an opportunity to share your thoughts, clarify doubts and connect with others in the human data field.

#### **Who should attend?**

- Researchers and professionals interested in **ELIXIR human data activities**
- Those looking to understand how to get involved with ELIXIR Communities
- Anyone in the UK working with human data

---

#### **Why attend?**

Based on experience from the **ELIXIR-UK All Hands**, there is increasing interest in the human data space to engage with ELIXIR, but uncertainty on how to start. This call will provide clarity on:

- The role of ELIXIR Communities
- Opportunities for participation
- Practical guidance for entry and collaboration
