This UK Human Data & Tech Club community call will introduce two platforms for genetic scores – **PGS Catalog** and **OmicsPred** – and the standards and infrastructure that keep them useful across research and clinical settings.

### Speaker

**Laurent Gil**
Principal Software Developer, *Wellcome Sanger Institute*

### What to expect

Genetic scores summarise an individual's genetic predisposition to a trait or disease, and their value depends on being findable, comparable and reproducible across studies.

- What genetic scores are and how they're used in research
- The standardised metadata and file format that keep scores comparable across studies and platforms
- The infrastructure and user interfaces behind PGS Catalog and OmicsPred
- A software developer's role in building and maintaining both projects

### Who is this for

This session is particularly relevant if you:

- Work with genetic or polygenic scores in your research
- Build or maintain research data platforms
- Are interested in metadata and file format standardisation
- Develop cloud infrastructure – websites, REST APIs – for research tools
