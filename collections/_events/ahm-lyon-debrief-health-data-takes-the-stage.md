Two workshops. One community. A growing impact.

The ELIXIR All Hands Meeting (AHM) is one of the most **competitive spaces for a slot** in the ELIXIR calendar of events – and this year, the leads of the Human Data & Tech Club have earned not one but **two workshops** on the programme. Securing two slots is real evidence of the relevance of the work of our members in our UK community.

### What's happening in Lyon

**Workshop 1 – Monday 9 June**
*Bringing health data standards across Europe: From OMOP to GA4GH*
Chaired by Wendi Bacon, Tim Beck, and Saskia Lawson-Tovey, this session brings together ELIXIR Nodes to align on health data standards including the OMOP Common Data Model and GA4GH standards such as Beacon, Phenopackets, and Passports/AAI. Featuring real-world use cases from HDR UK's Cohort Discovery tool and the 5 Safes TES, plus lightning talks from 14 ELIXIR Nodes and collaborative breakout discussions.

**Workshop 2 – Tuesday 10 June**
*Connecting health data pan-Europe: how can we help each other?*
Also chaired by Saskia Lawson-Tovey, Wendi Bacon, and Tim Beck, this session takes a community-building focus. With lightning talks from over 20 ELIXIR Nodes, it will surface shared challenges around GDPR, sensitive data infrastructure, and governance – and explore how Nodes can better support each other as the dedicated Health Data Focus Group winds down.

### What to expect

Following the AHM, our HDT Club call will hear directly from those who led and participated in Lyon. We'll share:

- Key takeaways and outcomes from both workshops
- Highlights from the node lightning talks across Europe
- Next steps for cross-node collaboration and community connection
- Plans for the post-workshop reports

Whether you were in Lyon or not, this is your chance to stay connected with the health data community's direction of travel across ELIXIR.

### Who is this for

This community call is for anyone with an interest in health data research and infrastructure across ELIXIR and beyond. You'll find it especially relevant if you:

- Are a member of the ELIXIR-UK Human Data & Tech Club or curious about joining.
- Work with health or clinical data in a research context.
- Are interested in pan-European data standards such as OMOP, GA4GH Beacon, or Phenopackets.
- Want to understand how different countries (ELIXIR Nodes) are navigating shared challenges around GDPR, sensitive data and governance.
- Couldn't make it to Lyon and want to stay connected with what's shaping the health data community space.
