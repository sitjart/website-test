Join the Club call with [Geert vanGeest](https://www.sib.swiss/meet-geert-van-geest-bioinformatics-trainer), ELIXIR Switzerland to discover how you can use the [ELIXIR training lesson template](https://github.com/elixir-europe-training/ELIXIR-TrP-LessonTemplate-MkDocs) for your future materials.
Geert is a Bioinformatics trainer at SIB (Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics) and the co-lead of the ELIXIR FAIR Training handbook project.

### **Who should attend**

Anyone creating open access training materials for on-demand training.
