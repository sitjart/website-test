The FAIR-in-action Bridge is a BioFAIR Pathfinder project led by the ELIXIR-UK RDM Club Coordinators to co-create two facilitator-ready playbooks: How to approach FAIRification and How to communicate and advocate FAIR.

[Find out more about the project.](/project/fair-in-action-bridge/)

These playbooks will translate existing FAIR guidance into practical, reusable activities, templates and examples that RDM professionals can adapt and deploy locally.

This first contentathon is a scoping session.

We want your voice in defining what each playbook should contain. We'll be asking:

- What does each playbook need to cover?
- Where are the current gaps between FAIR guidance and day-to-day practice?
- What structure would make these playbooks easy to pick up and adapt?
- Which existing resources do you have that should be embedded?

It's a drop-in format, so come for as long as you can. Whether you have five minutes of opinions or two hours of ideas, we'll be glad to have you.
