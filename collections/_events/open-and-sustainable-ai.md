AI assets – including **models and datasets** – are increasingly fundamental research outputs in need of good data management practices to remain **reusable, reproducible and sustainable** over time.

In this RDM Club Call, **[Gavin Farrell](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gavin-farrell97/) (ELIXIR Ireland & ELIXIR Italy)**, a contributor to [ELIXIR’s **AI Ecosystem** Focus Group work](https://elixir-europe.org/focus-groups/ai-ecosystem), will introduce the concept of **Open and Sustainable AI (OSAI)**. Grounded in the ELIXIR Europe Machine Learning Focus Group work that culminated in the 2025 preprint *[Open and Sustainable AI: challenges, opportunities and the road ahead in the life sciences](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16619)*, the talk presents **nine OSAI guidelines** for ensuring AI datasets and models can be reused and reproduced while also supporting **environmental sustainability**.

The session will also highlight a **living landscape of [300+ supports](https://osai.dome-ml.org/ai-ecosystem)** available for community application, designed to help implement OSAI guidelines in practice.

### Speaker

**Gavin Farrell** – *ELIXIR Ireland & ELIXIR Italy, contributor to ELIXIR’s AI Ecosystem focus group*

### Who should attend

This session is designed for professionals across the research, including but not limited to:

- **Research Data Managers and Research Software Quality Managers:** those looking to standardise AI workflows and integrate asset management into institutional/network-wide infrastructure.
- **AI Experimenters and Researchers:** individuals who need practical frameworks to make their AI code and datasets more reusable, reproducible, sustainable, and open.
- **Data Stewards:** professionals interested in applying community vetted guidelines and contributing new domain-specific implementations to the wider ecosystem.
