The annual ELIXIR-UK All Hands brings together people working at ELIXIR-UK consortium member institutions for this two-day meeting, which for 2018 is hosted by Birmingham University.

### What is the workshop about?

The annual ELIXIR-UK All Hands brings together people working at ELIXIR-UK consortium member institutions to:

1. Share knowledge and experience from the previous year of ELIXIR activities
2. Network with other people working on life science data infrastructure across the UK
3. Work together to develop a strategy for the future of ELIXIR-UK

### Target audience

All members of ELIXIR-UK consortium member institutions working on ELIXIR-related projects are welcome to join the two-day meeting, which this year is hosted by Birmingham University.
