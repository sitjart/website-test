<p>ELIXIR CONVERGE has released one of its main outputs, the RDMKit. This project has been co-led by ELIXIR-UK alongside ELIXIR-BE and ELIXIR-NO.</p>

<p>This new tool for empowering researchers in their data management has been a large undertaking and is an important resource which will cater to researchers' individual needs in a wide range of life science disciplines.&nbsp;</p>

<p>The RDMKit is a community-driven project and welcomes contributions, especially those which expand the disciplines covered. <a href="https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/how_to_contribute.html">For more information, visit the webpage on how to contribute.</a></p>
