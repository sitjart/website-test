<p>ELIXIR-UK contributed to two FAIRification events at ECCB2026, 31 August -&nbsp; 4 September in Geneva, Switzerland: a hands-on workshop within the ELIXIR Communities track and a satellite day comparing the <a href="https://github.com/elixir-europe/interoperability_2024-26/blob/FAIRificationProcess/pages/framework/FAIR_Resource_FAIRification_Framework_v0.2.md">ELIXIR</a>, <a href="https://www.go-fair.org/how-to-go-fair/fair-implementation-profile/">GO-FAIR</a> and <a href="https://codata.org/cross-domain-interoperability-framework-cdif-discovery-module-v01-draft-for-public-consultation/">CODATA</a> approaches.</p>

<p>The workshop, "ELIXIR-W2: Practical and pragmatic FAIRification of data in biodiversity, AI, health and beyond", was delivered by the ELIXIR Interoperability Platform representatives from the UK, Sweden and Switzerland with a community representative from the ELIXIR-Netherlands (Jente Houweling). Nick Juty, The University of Manchester represented ELIXIR-UK. The session demonstrated the FAIRification process (how to apply the FAIR principles – Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, Reusable), introduced the Platform's website, currently in development, and worked through a use case from the Toxicology Community. This was followed by a bring-your-own-data session. Attendees included ELIXIR community and platform representatives, none of whom had previously used the Framework.<br></p>

<figure class="wp-block-image alignfull size-large"><img src="https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/dsc_7308_dxo-715x1024.jpg" alt="image of Nick Juty at a table communicating to someone in the audience" class="wp-image-25385"/></figure>

<p>In addition, the satellite ELIXIR <a href="https://eccb2026.org/communities-day">Communities Day</a>, "From FAIR principles to data pipelines", set out to find touching points between the established FAIRification approaches. Morning sessions introduced each approach to the broader&nbsp; audience with practical implementation use cases. In the afternoon, ELIXIR representatives applied their FAIRification Process to a GO-FAIR use case and <em>vice versa</em>. They found that CODATA's Cross-Domain Interoperability Framework operates at a slightly higher level, where it can be incorporated at later stages for broader alignment, while the GO-FAIR and ELIXIR approaches align earlier and more readily, diverging only in later phases..</p>

<p>Representatives confirmed an interest in contributing to each other's existing calls as a route to early alignment, and identified RDA, EOSC task forces and COST actions as venues where the cross-work could continue.&nbsp;</p>

<p>The Platform has set out an ambition to produce downloadable bundles so that groups can run the FAIRification activities on their own use cases without a facilitator. UK groups wanting to apply the Framework to their own data can contact the <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/interoperability">Interoperability Platform</a>.</p>
