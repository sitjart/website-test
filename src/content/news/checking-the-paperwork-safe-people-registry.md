<p>Members of the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/activities/uk-human-data-community/" data-type="activity" data-id="18994">ELIXIR-UK Human Data Community</a> have highlighted the recent soft launch of the <a href="https://safepeopleregistry.org/"><strong>Safe People Registry</strong></a>, an open-source tool developed by HDR UK to support ‘safe people’ access validation for sensitive and secure data. The registry aligns with the <a href="https://fivesafes.org/">Five Safes principles</a>, enabling data services to provide safe research access to data.</p>

<p>The Safe People Registry provides a shared platform where individuals and their affiliated organisations can create profiles that bring together relevant information to support ‘safe people’ assessments.</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/banner_safe-registry-window-1024x576.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-24434" style="width:725px;height:auto"/></figure>

<p>It is designed to streamline processes used across Trusted Research Environments (TREs), Secure Data Environments (SDEs) and Safe Havens, reducing duplication and administrative burden for data custodians</p>

<figure class="wp-block-image size-large is-resized"><img src="https://elixiruknode.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/illustration-safe-registry-hdr-uk-1-1024x576.webp" alt="" class="wp-image-24445" style="width:725px;height:auto"/></figure>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<p>ELIXIR-UK’s involvement focuses on <strong>community engagement</strong> and <strong>knowledge exchange</strong>. Through the ELIXIR-UK Human Data Community, members have had a forum to discuss emerging approaches to accreditation, governance practices and tooling that support safe access to sensitive data.</p>

<p>ELIXIR-UK supports the open development approach taken by the Safe People Registry team and the sharing of design decisions, roadmaps and lessons learned with the wider community.</p>

<p>Development, operation and governance of the Safe People Registry sit entirely with the <strong>HDR UK team </strong>(<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/hdr-uk/">members of ELIXIR-UK since 2023</a>).</p>

<p>As part of their open development process, the Safe People Registry team has published a series of public software walkthroughs and a roadmap presentation outlining planned features and opportunities for feedback.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="hdr-uk-is-looking-for-trialling-projects">HDR UK is looking for trialling projects</h3>

<p>The Safe People Registry is currently in a soft-launch phase and welcomes expressions of interest from UK-based TREs, SDEs, and Safe Havens <strong>to trial the system</strong>.</p>

<p>Enquiries and trial requests should be directed to the Safe People Registry team at <a href="mailto:enquiries@safepeopleregistry.org">enquiries@safepeopleregistry.org</a>.</p>
