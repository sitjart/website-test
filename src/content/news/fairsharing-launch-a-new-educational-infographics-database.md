<p><a href="https://fairsharing.org/">FAIRsharing</a> is a manually curated, informative resource on data and metadata standards, inter-related databases and data policies. FAIRsharing is an <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/interoperability/rirs">ELIXIR Recommended Interoperability Resource</a> and an intrinsic registry of the EOSC and the ELIXIR RDM know-how ecosystem.&nbsp;</p>

<p>They have now launched their <a href="https://fairsharing.org/educational">FAIRsharing Educational</a> component, this fantastic resource is a set of infographics and factsheets covering FAIRsharing content and functionalities for all users and stakeholders across all disciplines.&nbsp;</p>

<p>This will be updated as an ongoing process, but the first six educational materials created by the FAIRsharing team and the <a href="https://fairsharing.org/community_champions">FAIRsharing Community Champions</a> are now available.&nbsp;</p>

<p>They are:</p>

<ol>
<li><a href="https://fairsharing.org/educational#nutshell">FAIRsharing in a nutshell</a></li>

<li><a href="https://fairsharing.org/educational#standards">FAIRsharing content: standards overview</a></li>

<li><a href="https://fairsharing.org/educational#databases">FAIRsharing content: databases overview</a></li>

<li><a href="https://fairsharing.org/educational#researchers">FAIRsharing for you: researchers</a></li>

<li><a href="https://fairsharing.org/educational#funders">FAIRsharing for you: funders</a></li>

<li><a href="https://fairsharing.org/educational#publishers">FAIRsharing for you: journal publishers</a></li>
</ol>

<p>We are excited to see all the new content coming out of this essential initiative!</p>
