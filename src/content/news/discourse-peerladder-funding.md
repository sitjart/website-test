<p><strong>ELIXIR-UK </strong>members have been awarded funding through the DisCouRSE Flexible Fund to deliver <strong>PeerLadder</strong>, a new peer-mentoring initiative supporting leadership development among underrepresented digital Research Technical Professionals (dRTPs) across UK research-performing organisations.</p>

<p>PeerLadder will run from <strong>March 2026 to March 2027</strong> and is part of the first cohort of community-led projects funded by <a href="https://discourse-network.github.io/"><strong>DisCouRSE</strong></a>, a UKRI-funded Network+ focused on strengthening leadership, career pathways and recognition across the digital research workforce.</p>

<p>dRTPs play a critical role in enabling data-driven research, yet many face limited progression routes into leadership, particularly women, disabled people, LGBTQIA+ professionals and those from minority ethnic backgrounds, limiting diversity of thought and innovation (<a href="https://www.britishscienceassociation.org/news/briefing-on-equality-diversity-and-inclusion-strategies-in-stem-makes-business-case-for-growth">BSA briefing</a>, <a href="https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5803/cmselect/cmsctech/95/summary.html">House of Commons report</a>).</p>

<p>PeerLadder will address this gap through a structured peer-mentoring model that builds confidence, leadership capabilities, and sustained professional networks.</p>

<p>The project adapts lessons from<strong> </strong><a href="https://elixir-europe.org/how-we-work/scientific-programme/people/elead2">ELEAD</a>, ELIXIR’s established European leadership and mentoring programme for women, focusing on its most impactful element: peer mentoring. The UK pilot broadens the approach to include all underrepresented groups, not just gender. </p>

<p>PeerLadder is coordinated from the University of Manchester, led by<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/saskia-lawson-tovey/" data-type="people" data-id="18663"> Saskia Lawson-Tovey</a> and co-led by <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/xenia-perez-sitja/" data-type="people" data-id="18755">Xènia Pérez Sitjà</a> (<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/earlham-institute/" data-type="member" data-id="18575">Earlham Institute</a>), Sara Villa (<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/open-life-science/" data-type="member" data-id="18560">OLS</a>), and <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/carole-goble/" data-type="people" data-id="18640">Carole Goble</a> (<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/university-of-manchester/" data-type="member" data-id="18574">University of Manchester</a>), combining expertise in research infrastructure, community leadership, mentoring programme delivery, and equality, diversity, and inclusion.</p>

<p>Participants will attend an in-person workshop followed by monthly virtual peer-mentoring sessions, with support designed to enable the groups to continue beyond the funded period. Insights and outcomes from the pilot will feed back into <strong>DisCouRSE</strong>, <strong>ELIXIR-UK</strong>, and the <strong>Software Sustainability Institute</strong>, informing future approaches to inclusive leadership, mentoring, and career development across the UK digital research community.</p>
