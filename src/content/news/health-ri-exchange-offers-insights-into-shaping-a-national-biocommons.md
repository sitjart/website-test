<p>In October 2024, ELIXIR-UK and its conceived initiative – BioFAIR –  engaged in a knowledge exchange with the Netherlands' Health-RI (ELIXIR-NL) to refine governance and operational strategies for national research data infrastructures.</p>

<p>The meeting brought together <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/gerry-reilly/" data-type="people" data-id="22924">Gerry Reilly</a>, Interim Director of <a href="https://biofair.uk/">BioFAIR</a>, with Mijke Jetten, FAIR Data Lead, and Janet Vos, Coordinator of European Research Infrastructures at <a href="https://www.health-ri.nl/en" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow">Health-RI</a>/ELIXIR-NL, alongside <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/munazah-andrabi/" data-type="people" data-id="18649">Munazah Andrabi</a>, Community and Data Manager at the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/university-of-manchester/" data-type="member" data-id="18574">University of Manchester</a> (ELIXIR-UK), to discuss lessons learned and explore collaborative opportunities.</p>

<p>Health-RI, the Netherlands' established data infrastructure for health and life sciences, provides a comprehensive “National Data Portal” with a suite of services that support data-driven research across disciplines. As BioFAIR begins to establish a similar infrastructure for UK researchers, Health-RI's insights on governance and model development proved valuable, highlighting shared challenges and effective strategies.</p>

<p>Key collaboration areas emerged from the discussions, including metadata model development, cataloguing, and best practice sharing. Health-RI's established workflows in these domains offer BioFAIR a potential foundation for adapting proven methods and tools. The two initiatives also explored joint training opportunities, envisioning shared training materials and resources to support FAIR data compliance across borders.</p>

<p>Through this exchange, BioFAIR gains access to Health-RI's tested approaches to establishing a national BioCommons, strengthening the UK’s capability to build a robust data infrastructure tailored to the needs of its life science research community.</p>
