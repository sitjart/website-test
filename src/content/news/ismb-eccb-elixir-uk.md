<p>From 20-24 July 2025, the international bioinformatics and computational biology community gathered in Liverpool for the biannual ISMB/ECCB – and ELIXIR-UK was there with a visible and collaborative presence.</p>

<p>Across talks, posters, booth activities and thematic tracks, ELIXIR-UK members showcased how FAIR data practices, open tools and community-driven platforms are shaping the future of life science research.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="co-hosting-the-bioinformatics-in-the-uk-track"><strong>Co-hosting the “Bioinformatics in the UK” track</strong></h2>

<p style="padding-top:0;padding-bottom:0">ELIXIR-UK co-organised the “<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/event/bioinformatics-in-the-uk-ismb-eccb-2025/">Bioinformatics in the UK</a>” track on the second day, chaired by <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/carole-goble/" data-type="people" data-id="18640">Carole Goble</a> (ELIXIR-UK Joint Head of Node) and Janet Thornton. The track was sponsored by HDR UK, AstraZeneca UK, BioFAIR, UCB, ELIXIR-UK, AIBIO-UK and Astex Pharmaceuticals.</p>

<p>It spotlighted the role of national infrastructures, emerging tools and collaborative strategies that keep the UK at the forefront of life science bioinformatics.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="a-collaborative-uk-trail-and-an-engaging-booth"><strong>A collaborative UK Trail and an engaging booth</strong></h2>

<p>For the first time, ELIXIR-UK partnered with AIBIO-UK, HDR UK and BioFAIR to create a UK Trail across the exhibition floor. Visitors were invited to collect stamps and discover what each booth had to offer,&nbsp; with over 200 attendees participating. This collaborative approach brought consistent traffic and fresh conversations across all four UK-led stands.</p>

<p>We took advantage of this attention to offer contributors and members from across our national Node to share materials and demos of the services and initiatives we endorse:</p>
