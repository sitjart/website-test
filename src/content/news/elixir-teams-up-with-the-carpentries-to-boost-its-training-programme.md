<p>ELIXIR and <a href="https://carpentries.org/">The Carpentries</a> initiative, a merged organization of Software Carpentry and Data Carpentry, have agreed to extend their collaboration in organising and delivering training in ELIXIR Nodes. As part of the collaboration, The Carpentries will organise close to 20 Software and Data Carpentry training workshops across ELIXIR Nodes, and two Instructor Training workshops to train a number of specialised instructors.</p>

<p>This collaboration is funded by EU Horizon 2020 project <a href="https://www.elixir-europe.org/about-us/how-funded/eu-projects/excelerate">ELIXIR-EXCELERATE</a> (contract no. 676559) as part of the training activities (WP11).</p>
