<p>On 17 July 2025, ELIXIR-UK joined leaders from EMBL-EBI, the Pistoia Alliance, and the wider ELIXIR for a high-level strategy meeting held at the EMBL-EBI campus in Hinxton, UK.</p>

<p>The meeting explored new models for sustainable collaboration between commercial R&amp;D, public research infrastructures, and supply-side organisations. Carole Goble, ELIXIR-UK Head of Node, played a leading role in the agenda and discussions throughout the day. She led a breakout session on i<strong>nteroperability and cross-industry collaboration</strong> and chaired the final panel session on <strong>building a sustainable collaboration framework that included funding requirements</strong>. </p>

<p>On this event, the UK Node was happy to attend and participate in discussions alongside our colleagues at <strong>BioFAIR</strong>, the BioCommons infrastructure for UK life science researchers. <strong>Tony Burdett</strong>, Director at BioFAIR, contributed to the panel discussion on sustainable collaboration. </p>

<p>This engagement forms part of ELIXIR-UK’s broader mission to ensure UK contributions to <strong>FAIR life sciences research infrastructures</strong> are well-represented in international conversations – particularly at the intersection of policy, technology and long-term impact and sustainability. </p>

<p>For more on the event, <a href="https://www.pistoiaalliance.org/eventdetails/pistoia-alliance-embl-ebi-and-elixir/">visit Pistoia Alliance’s website</a>.</p>
