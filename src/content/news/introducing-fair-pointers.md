<p>The <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/activities/elixir-dash-fellowship/">ELIXIR-UK DaSH Fellowship</a> aims to embed Research Data Management (RDM) know-how into UK universities and institutes, the Fellows are creating training and delivering local training as part of this.&nbsp;</p>

<p>A new training course that has been co-written with ELIXIR-UK members and Fellows is now available; FAIR Pointers is a short, online course that is catered towards those with little or no experience around ‘FAIR data’ and how that can be intertwined into research practice.</p>

<p>This course covers the origins of FAIR, the <a href="https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/">FAIR Principles</a> and their characteristics. By signposting to other resources, this course will allow for a well-rounded education on the subject. These other resources include <a href="https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/">RDMkit</a>, <a href="https://faircookbook.elixir-europe.org/content/home.html">FAIRCookbook</a>, <a href="https://fellowship.elixiruknode.org/rdmbites">RDMbites</a>, <a href="https://fairsharing.org/">FAIRsharing</a> and the <a href="https://ds-wizard.org/">Data Stewardship Wizard</a>.</p>

<p>The course is free, and available to be taken at the users' own pace on their own schedule. There are exercises within sections, which is a great way to ingrain the knowledge learnt into memory throughout.</p>
