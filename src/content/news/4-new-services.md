<p>Following the 2022 call for new services, we are pleased to announce four new ELIXIR-UK services. These were selected by our panel to ensure high service quality and match UK priorities as identified by its funders.</p>

<p>The successful services were: <a href="https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org">RDMkit </a>[University of Manchester], <a href="https://www.cafevariome.org/">Cafe Variome</a> [University of Leicester], <a href="http://missense3d.bc.ic.ac.uk/">Missense 3D</a> [Imperial College London] and <a href="https://www.variantvalidator.org/">Variant Validator</a> [ University of Manchester].</p>

<p>We received several high-quality applications and are proud to be representing the best of the UK life science data resources. The successful services will now benefit from their status as ELIXIR-UK services and will be able to apply for funding from the ELIXIR Hub and beyond. </p>

<p>We have also roadmapped three services, <a href="https://mabra.biomed.kcl.ac.uk/BRepertoire_6/">BRepertoire</a> [Kings College London], <a href="https://cyverseuk.org/">Cyverse-UK</a> [Earlham Institute] and <a href="https://www.dundee.ac.uk/hic">Health Informatics Centre (HIC)</a> [University of Dundee] which are well on their way to being infrastructure-ready. </p>
