<p>A suite of new and enhanced resources to support Research Data Management (RDM) services is now available, and the ELIXIR-UK community has been involved in their development.</p>

<p>These resources are the result of ELIXIR's first internally funded project for its <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/communities/research-data-management">RDM Community</a> at the European level. Several ELIXIR-UK members led tasks throughout the project, with the RDM Community currently co-led by UK member <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/munazah-andrabi/" data-type="people" data-id="18649">Munazah Andrabi</a>.</p>

<p>Good RDM is at the heart of the FAIR principles – making data Findable, Accessible, Interoperable and Reusable – and underpins open science more broadly. These resources, developed by RDM experts from across ELIXIR Nodes, grow ELIXIR's distributed knowledge, training and services.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="enhancements-to-tools-you-already-use">Enhancements to tools you already use</h2>

<p>Key updates have been made to resources already familiar to the ELIXIR-UK community:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong><a href="https://rdmkit.elixir-europe.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">RDMkit</a></strong>&nbsp;– community-maintained guidance for life sciences RDM was expanded and refreshed.</li>

<li><strong><a href="https://faircookbook.elixir-europe.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">FAIR Cookbook</a></strong>&nbsp;– new and updated practical recipes to support FAIR data implementation.</li>

<li><strong><a href="https://tess.elixir-europe.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">TeSS</a></strong> – a new Data Management for Researchers learning path was added, co-developed with the ELIXIR Learning Paths focus group and Training Platform, with a Data Stewardship learning path also in the works, led by UK member, <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/alexia-cardona/" data-type="people" data-id="18643">Alexia Cardona</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="a-new-handbook-for-rdm-professionals">A new handbook for RDM professionals</h2>

<p>One of the most significant outputs is the&nbsp;<strong><a href="https://elixir-europe.github.io/ds-handbook/index" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Data Stewardship Handbook</a></strong>&nbsp;– aimed at those delivering institutional RDM services, rather than the researchers using them. UK member <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/xenia-perez-sitja/" data-type="people" data-id="18755">Xenia Perez Sitja</a> has been involved in the development of this handbook, with guidance pages and real-world community case studies that complement the operational focus of the RDMkit.</p>

<p>The handbook also includes an&nbsp;<strong><a href="https://elixir-europe.github.io/ds-handbook/maturity-model" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">RDM Services Maturity Model</a></strong>&nbsp;– a shared framework to help ELIXIR Nodes and other institutions assess their current state and identify practical next steps across four domains: strategy and sustainability, legal and governance, RDM support, and data and metadata management.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="clearer-pathways-for-data-deposition">Clearer pathways for data deposition</h2>

<p>New&nbsp;<strong><a rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank" href="https://zenodo.org/records/17979335">recommendations</a></strong>&nbsp;address how data stewards support researchers in depositing data to ELIXIR Deposition Databases, proposing clearer recognition of brokering roles, improved authentication, better cross-repository linking and more machine-actionable repository requirements.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="a-stronger-more-connected-community">A stronger, more connected community</h2>

<p>Alongside these resources, a&nbsp;<strong><a href="https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15235046" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">community framework</a></strong>&nbsp;and onboarding materials have been developed to help sustain and grow the ELIXIR RDM Community. The collective effort marks a shift away from isolated, ad hoc data management towards more standardised practices – meaning less data sitting on hard drives and more available to be found, understood and reused with confidence.</p>
