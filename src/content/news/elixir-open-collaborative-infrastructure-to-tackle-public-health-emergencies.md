<p><strong>The rapid spread of the COVID-19 virus highlights the need for an open analytics environment so that the global research community can collaborate on developing effective countermeasures.&nbsp;</strong></p>

<p>27 February, Hinxton, UK.</p>

<p>Open-access data and open, reproducible analytics are crucial for a fast and efficient response to global health crises and can significantly contribute to our understanding of novel viruses. This is the main point of a joint paper by Galaxy teams from the United States, Australia and ELIXIR, who re-analysed available COVID-19 data and assessed the reproducibility of initial papers on the COVID-19 genome.</p>
