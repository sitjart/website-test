<p>On March 6th and 7th, members of ELIXIR-UK (Carole Goble, University of Manchester, Nicola Soranzo, Earlham Institute and Gos Micklem, University of Cambridge) attended the <a href="https://www.ukri.org/">UKRI</a> Digital Research Infrastructure (DRI) Community Congress. This year marked 5 years since the work was undertaken in the <a href="https://www.ukri.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/10/UKRI-201020-UKinfrastructure-opportunities-to-grow-our-capacity-FINAL.pdf">UKRI Infrastructure Roadmap</a>.</p>

<p>Invited to this meeting were a wide range of participants, including representatives from government, industry, and across the breadth of the research and innovation DRI community.</p>

<p>The content of this two-day event covered the five themes or the DRI strategy. These are:</p>

<ul>
<li>Data infrastructure</li>

<li>Large-scale compute</li>

<li>Secure services and tools for sensitive data</li>

<li>Skills and career pathways</li>

<li>Foundational tools, techniques and practices</li>
</ul>

<p>We presented a poster for <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/project/biofair/" data-type="project" data-id="18829">BioFAIR</a> during the two-day Congress, and Carole delivered a plenary reflection response.</p>

<p>We look forward to attending any further Community Congress events!</p>
