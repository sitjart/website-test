<p class="has-medium-font-size">Members of ELIXIR-UK attended in force the 2024 ELIXIR Europe All Hands meeting in Uppsala, Sweden, during the week of June 13th, 2024, to discuss collaborative solutions to life sciences data challenges and align strategies with the new ELIXIR scientific programme.</p>

<p>This year, 25 members of ELIXIR-UK from 13 different organisations attended the All Hands meeting in person, leading or presenting in nearly 60% of all sessions and numerous posters. Such great representation highlights the diversity and collaborative nature of the UK Node’s work across all scientific areas of ELIXIR.</p>

<p>With a focus on generating impact and long-lasting connections, UK Node members attended this meeting from multiple organisations, at different career stages and with diverse interests, from infrastructure development to strategic partnerships.</p>

<blockquote class="wp-block-quote">
<p>“The ELIXIR UK Node is such a vibrant and productive member of the ELIXIR community, consistently demonstrating leadership across numerous all-hands sessions. We are proud of our members and the energy they bring to this European network”</p>
<cite><strong>Prof Carole Goble, Joint Head of Node</strong></cite></blockquote>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="the-focus-of-the-uk-node-at-the-all-hands"><strong>The focus of the UK Node at the All Hands</strong></h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="international-collaboration">International collaboration</h3>

<div class="wp-block-greenshift-blocks-row gspb_row gspb_row-id-gsbp-fdfae83 gspb_row--gutter-30" id="gspb_row-id-gsbp-fdfae83"><div class="gspb_row__content"> 
<div class="wp-block-greenshift-blocks-row-column gspb_row__col--6 gspb_col-id-gsbp-feb2056" id="gspb_col-id-gsbp-feb2056">
<p>International collaboration was a significant focus, with Gerry Reilly, Interim Director of <a href="https://biofair.uk/">BioFAIR</a>, and Eva Wan, Project Manager of BioFAIR, presenting on "Collaborative pathways for BioFAIR UK, ELIXIR, and European Life Science." This workshop discussed collaboration between national infrastructure projects, featuring presentations from collaborators at the <a href="https://www.biocommons.org.au/">Australian BioCommons</a> and <a href="https://www.researchspace.com/">RSpace</a>.</p>
</div>

Meanwhile, Prof <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/susanna-assunta-sansone/" data-type="people" data-id="18662">Susanna-Assunta Sansone</a> participated in a panel discussion about ELIXIR’s impact through international partnerships, emphasising the importance of building connections and collaborations on a global scale.</p>

<div class="wp-block-greenshift-blocks-row-column gspb_row__col--6 gspb_col-id-gsbp-a4b36b6" id="gspb_col-id-gsbp-a4b36b6">
<p>Artificial intelligence and text mining were prominently featured. Dr Valerie Wood, University of Cambridge, presented "Supporting biocuration with AI and LLMs," unveiling what curators need in this evolving landscape, while Dr <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/tim-beck/" data-type="people" data-id="18652">Tim Beck</a>, University of Nottingham,&nbsp; led a workshop on "Text mining to support systematic literature reviews," discussing scalable curation.</p>
</div>
 </div></div>

<p>Dr Barbara Szomolay, Cardiff University,&nbsp; and Prof <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/krzysztof-poterlowicz/" data-type="people" data-id="18756">Krzysztof Poterlowicz</a>, University of Bradford, also led a workshop on "Paving the way towards the effective use of generative AI for ELIXIR." It included insights from invited speakers from AstraZeneca, University of Mannheim and West Virginia University on using ChatGPT and AI for drug development, bioinformatics, educational content, and dataset assessment.&nbsp;</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="data-integration-and-interoperability">Data Integration and interoperability</h3>

<p>Data integration and interoperability were key themes, with Dr <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/nick-juty/" data-type="people" data-id="18618">Nick Juty</a>, University of Manchester, and Prof Susanna-Assunta Sansone, University of Oxford, leading sessions on the future of data integrations and interoperability for multiomics. In the workshop titled&nbsp; “The future of data integration – shaping ELIXIR’s Interoperability Platform with real-world use cases”, co-led by Nick,&nbsp; over 80 participants made significant strides towards the new ELIXIR programme. They aimed to provide practical FAIRification for ELIXIR Communities.</p>

<p>The workshop concluded with an exciting announcement: the first face-to-face event where communities and researchers can engage with interoperability platform activities will be held at <a href="https://eccb2024.fi/workshops-tutorials">ECCB</a> as part of the ELIXIR track. This event, titled "Practical and pragmatic FAIRification targeting ELIXIR Communities," invites any Community to participate in FAIRification efforts.</p>

<p>Crucially, the workshop not only focused on onboarding Communities for interoperability tasks but also established connections with other key areas of ELIXIR. Research Data Management (RDM) community. Cross-collaboration with RDM and training activities within ELIXIR will be instrumental in scaling up in the long term.&nbsp;</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="single-omics-galaxy-and-environmental-impact">Single omics, Galaxy and environmental impact</h3>

<p>Dr <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/wendi-bacon/" data-type="people" data-id="18624">Wendi Bacon</a>, The Open University, excelled at the workshop she led on "Tackling single-cell data management and interoperability in Galaxy." The workshop discussed challenges in data management, using public data, and getting trainees to analyse their own data. This work connects with the <a href="https://galaxyproject.org/community/sig/singlecell/">Galaxy Single-cell and Spatial Omics community</a>, where anyone is invited to get involved.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>

<p>Also on Galaxy, Dr <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/nicola-soranzo/" data-type="people" data-id="18644">Nicola Soranzo</a> co-led a workshop titled "Where is the Dark Matter? The Galaxy Community shedding light on activities, connections, scope and unaddressed shortcomings" featuring world café discussions on RDM, computational workflows, and training.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="training-and-education">Training and education</h3>

<p>In the realm of training and education, ELIXIR-UK had a strong representation.&nbsp; Dr <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/alexia-cardona/" data-type="people" data-id="18643">Alexia Cardona</a> discussed "Training standards and best practices for Open Science and Open Education," and led two roundtable discussions on learning pathways and standardising training material formats. Prof Krzysztof Poterlowicz addressed the consolidation of the ELIXIR Train-the-Trainer programme and, alongside Dr Barbara Szomolay, led a roundtable on leveraging large language models for training.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="about-the-elixir-europe-all-hands"><strong>About the ELIXIR Europe All Hands</strong></h4>

<p>ELIXIR Europe has celebrated its All Hands Meeting every year since 2015. Each year, a Node hosts an event that welcomes all members of the pan-European community to join this annual event, build connections, reinforce existing ones, and be the conduit for future projects. All members, including prospective members and key collaborators, are invited to attend the annual event. This year, the ELIXIR Swedish Node hosted the event at the beautiful building of Uppsala University, with the prospects of a warmer set-up for 2025 in Thessaloniki, hosted by ELIXIR Greece.&nbsp;</p>

<p>For more information about the ELIXIR All Hands meeting, visit the <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/news/AHM-2024">ELIXIR Europe news release</a>.&nbsp;</p>
