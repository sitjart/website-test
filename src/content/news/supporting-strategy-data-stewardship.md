<p>In May 2025, ELIXIR-UK members <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/xenia-perez-sitja/" data-type="people" data-id="18755">Xènia Pérez Sitjà</a> (<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/earlham-institute/" data-type="member" data-id="18575">Earlham Institute</a>) and <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/nick-juty/" data-type="people" data-id="18618">Nick Juty</a> (<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/university-of-manchester/" data-type="member" data-id="18574">University of Manchester</a>) took part in the <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/platforms/training/elitma">ELITMa course</a> on data management strategy for ELIXIR Nodes, organised by the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/activities/rdm-community/" data-type="activity" data-id="20925">ELIXIR Research Data Management (RDM) Community</a> at the University of Tartu, Estonia. </p>

<p>Xènia attended in her role as co-lead of several RDM Community activities and as a key contributor to the upcoming <em>Data Stewardship Handbook</em> – a new resource designed to support the work of data stewards across Europe. Nick joined as a member of the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/activities/interoperability-platform/" data-type="activity" data-id="19885">ELIXIR Interoperability Platform</a>, with a focus on aligning technical efforts and fostering deeper collaboration between the Platform and the RDM Community.</p>

<p>The course generated vibrant discussion and set clear goals for future collaboration, including the development of cross-domain use cases and the finalisation of the handbook as a ready-to-use resource.</p>

<p>This work is particularly relevant to the UK, where the role of data stewards is growing rapidly. The Node has a strong interest in supporting and professionalising this critical part of the research workforce – ensuring the contributions of Digital Research Technical Professionals are recognised and that talent is retained across UK research-performing organisations.</p>

<p>We thank our colleagues Heleri Inno (ELIXIR Estonia) and Mijke Jetten (ELIXIR Netherlands) for their outstanding coordination and leadership in driving the RDM Community forward.</p>
