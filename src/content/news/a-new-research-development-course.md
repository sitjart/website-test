<p>The <a href="https://www.manchester.ac.uk/">University of Manchester</a> and <a href="https://software.ac.uk/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">the Software Sustainability Institute (SSI)</a> are piloting a new <a href="https://uomresearchit.github.io/python-intermediate-development-nerc/index.html" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">course on intermediate research software skills for Earth, atmospheric and oceanic sciences</a> at a <a href="https://uk-carpentries.github.io/2023-03-27-manchester-intermediate-swc/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">workshop from 27 to 31 March 2023</a> at the University of Manchester.</p>

<p>The course is based on the SSI's <a href="https://carpentries-incubator.github.io/python-intermediate-development/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Intermediate Research Software Skill in Python course</a> from the <a href="https://github.com/carpentries-incubator/python-intermediate-development" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Carpentries Lesson Incubator</a>. </p>

<p>The target audience is people who:</p>

<ul>
<li>Write code in research environments </li>

<li>Have gained basic software development skills </li>

<li>Are seeing their software projects now becoming larger and involving more researchers and other stakeholders</li>

<li>Are noticing the software is becoming more complex and there are more users and an increasing need to add new functionality</li>

<li>Now need more intermediate software engineering techniques and tools to develop manage and release their software for reuse by others</li>
</ul>

<p>Luckily, this new course will cover all of the above!</p>

<p>The workshop will consist of instructor-led code-along sessions, individual self-learning sessions aided by helpers, and small-group exercises mimicking real-life collaborative software projects. At the workshop, they will be joined by colleagues from the University of Bradford and Earlham Institute who are looking to deliver the <a href="https://carpentries-incubator.github.io/python-intermediate-development/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">original course</a> for <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ELIXIR-UK</a> and <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">ELIXIR</a> audiences.</p>

<p>Find out more <a href="https://research-it.manchester.ac.uk/news/2022/11/30/reproducible-environmental-research-from-research-it/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">about the course</a>.</p>
