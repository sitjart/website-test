<p><a href="http://www.pistoiaalliance.org/">The Pistoia Alliance</a>, a global, not-for-profit alliance that works to lower barriers to innovation in life sciences R&amp;D has today announced the launch of the <a href="http://uxls.org/">User Experience (UX) for Life Sciences (LS) Toolkit</a>, to help life science companies improve UX in line with other global industries.</p>

<p>The UXLS toolkit marks the culmination of a collaborative <a href="http://www.pistoiaalliance.org/projects/uxls/">project</a> involving more than 50 UX specialists from 20 different organisations across the world; including several top ten pharmaceutical companies, bioscience, and technology firms.</p>

<p>The toolkit contains UX case studies, methods, and metrics – enabling life science companies to design better, more intuitive, more usable digital products, specifically for R&amp;D in the life science and healthcare environment. </p>

<p>These issues and the benefits of UXLS for improving productivity will be discussed further at The Pistoia Alliance’s User Experience for Life Science <a href="http://www.pistoiaalliance.org/eventdetails/user-experience-life-science-conference/">conference</a> on the 14-15<sup>th</sup> May 2018 in Boston, USA, featuring keynote speakers from companies including Novartis.</p>
