<p>The ELIXIR UK team attended the ELIXIR All Hands Meeting, which was held in Berlin on 4-7 June 2018.</p>

<p>The All Hands&nbsp;brings together members of the ELIXIR community from across the ELIXIR Nodes, and collaborators from partner organisations, in order to review ELIXIR's achievements and activities so far and discuss plans for the future.</p>
