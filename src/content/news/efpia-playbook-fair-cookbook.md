<p>In July 2024, EFPIA, the European Federation of Pharmaceutical Industries and Association, officially released a comprehensive playbook to guide Innovative Medicines Initiative (IMI) and Innovative Health Initiative (IHI) projects implementing best data-sharing practices. Among its key recommendations, the playbook highlights the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/fair-cookbook/" data-type="service" data-id="18747">FAIR Cookbook</a>, a service of the ELIXIR-UK Node, co-run with and recognised by the wider ELIXIR community.</p>

<p>The EFPIA playbook, a culmination of extensive collaboration among researchers who have navigated the complexities of data sharing, offers a practical blueprint for effectively managing human and health data. It identifies common workflows and technical processes that address the challenges of data sharing, such as adhering to regulatory mandates.</p>

<p>It recommends using the FAIR Cookbook, a live “The Essential Resource by and for FAIR doers” &nbsp; that provides a step-by-step guide to making data Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable (FAIR).</p>

<p><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/susanna-assunta-sansone/" data-type="people" data-id="18662">Prof Susanna-Assunta Sansone</a> and <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/philippe-rocca-serra/" data-type="people" data-id="18616">Dr Philippe Rocca-Serra</a>, both key members of ELIXIR-UK, have championed the FAIR Cookbook, with colleagues from Luxemburg, Switzerland, Spain and The Netherlands, and in collaboration with the USA NIH <em>Office</em> of <em>Data</em> Science Strategy. Their work has been instrumental in establishing it as a trusted resource within the research community and funding agencies since the IMI/IHI Project Guidelines also recommend the FAIR Cookbook for “Open Access to Publication and Research Data, and Horizon Europe Work Programme for Health.</p>

<p>The recognition of the FAIR Cookbook in the EFPIA playbook underscores its importance as a tool for researchers and organisations engaged in making their data FAIR.&nbsp;For more information on the playbook and its recommendations,<a href="https://www.efpia.eu/news-events/the-efpia-view/blog-articles/data-sharing-playbook-can-unlock-health-partnerships/"> read the official EFPIA news release</a>.</p>
