<p>On 27 November 2025, ELIXIR-UK contributed to a national discussion on strengthening and coordinating training provision for digital Research Technical Professionals (dRTPs) at the <em>“</em><a href="https://www.tickettailor.com/events/stepupproject/1867117">Building a comprehensive and coordinated training landscape for dRTPs”</a> meeting held in London and organised by <a href="https://step-up.ac.uk/"><strong>STEP-UP</strong></a>.</p>

<p>Participation from ELIXIR-UK reflects ongoing work within the Node to support dRTPs in the life sciences through coordinated, high-quality training, skills development and career recognition. This includes established leadership in the area, through initiatives such as the ELIXIR-UK Data Stewardship Fellowship, which has played a key role in developing capacity, career pathways, and community-led training for data stewards and related roles across the UK life sciences research community.</p>

<p>ELIXIR-UK was represented through its training involvement, with <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/alexia-cardona/"><strong>Dr Alexia Cardona</strong></a> (ELIXIR-UK Training Platform Coordinator, University of Cambridge) attending and contributing perspectives informed by both national Node-level activity and ELIXIR’s broader European training infrastructure.</p>

<p>The meeting brought together organisations and initiatives involved in national training provision for digital research professionals, including <strong>The Carpentries</strong>, <strong>CaSDaR</strong>, <strong>DiRAC</strong>, <strong>UKRI</strong>, <strong>DisCouRSE</strong>, <strong>EMBL-EBI</strong>, <strong>HDR UK</strong>, and other UK training leads.</p>

<p>Discussions focused on shared challenges across the UK training landscape, including mapping competencies for dRTP roles, identifying gaps in existing provision, and improving coordination across programmes and infrastructures.</p>

<p>During the meeting, <strong>participants referenced and recognised ELIXIR training resources and collaborators</strong>. This included<a href="https://training.galaxyproject.org/"> <strong>Galaxy Training Network</strong></a>, with a strong ELIXIR involvement, <strong>&nbsp;</strong><a href="https://hdruklearn.org/"><strong>HDR-UK Futures</strong></a>, HDR-UK now a member of our Node, and <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/tess/"><strong>TeSS</strong></a>, ELIXIR’s training portal, which is led from the <strong>University of Manchester</strong> through ELIXIR-UK and plays a central role in improving the findability, accessibility and reusability of life science training across Europe and beyond.</p>

<p>This engagement highlights how ELIXIR-UK’s activities and leadership feed into, and are reinforced by, ELIXIR’s European-level infrastructure, supporting a more coherent, sustainable and life-science-focused training ecosystem for digital research professionals.</p>
