<p>On 1 March 2024, the EU-funded <a href="https://eosc-entrust.eu/">EOSC-ENTRUST</a> project launched to enhance European interoperability for sensitive data access and analysis. Led by <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/">ELIXIR</a> and the <a href="https://eudat.eu/eudat-cdi">EUDAT Collaborative Data Infrastructure (CDI)</a>, this collaborative effort involves partners from 15 European countries, including ELIXIR-UK members such as University of Dundee, University of Manchester, University of Nottingham and Health Data Research UK (HDR UK). With EU funding amounting to €4.2 million, EOSC-ENTRUST aims to address critical challenges over a three-year period.</p>

<p>At its core, EOSC-ENTRUST seeks to establish a European network of Trusted Research Environments (TREs) for sensitive data and develop a common blueprint for federated data access and analysis. These TREs provide secure platforms for handling sensitive datasets, enabling data privacy and legal compliance in research involving confidential information.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Europe's landscape of TREs is fragmented, presenting challenges in access and management for researchers and providers. The project aims to harmonise various systems and procedures for a unified approach to managing federated access across differing technologies and governance frameworks.</p>

<p>Leveraging the <a href="https://eosc-portal.eu/">European Open Science Cloud (EOSC)</a> Interoperability Framework, the project will address legal, technical and semantic interoperability challenges to enable seamless collaboration across disciplinary boundaries.&nbsp;</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="key-initiatives-by-the-uk-node"><strong>Key initiatives by the UK Node</strong></h2>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="delivering-the-blueprint-for-connecting-tres-and-use-cases">Delivering the blueprint for connecting TREs and use cases</h3>

<p><a href="https://www.hdruk.ac.uk/">HDR UK</a> will share knowledge of its well-established principles implemented by UK TREs and bring expertise in the building of consensus standards for multiple TREs. It will demonstrate the feasibility of data-enabled clinical trials and predictive models, supporting systematic/scoping reviews and the development of policy measures by combining different data types.</p>

<p>In this effort, the <a href="https://www.dundee.ac.uk/hic">Health Informatics Centre</a> at the University of Dundee also brings knowledge and technology for producing a TRE for over 12 years, supporting thousands of projects providing access to NHS Scotland data.&nbsp;</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="demonstrating-federated-analytics-across-tres">Demonstrating federated analytics across TREs</h3>

<p>Researchers seeking to perform analysis across TREs face a frustrating approach, with competing standards, competing data curation requests and very manual and slow methods. ELIXIR has the technology to wrap workflows with <a href="https://www.researchobject.org/ro-crate/">RO-Crate</a>. The <a href="https://www.nottingham.ac.uk/">University of Nottingham</a> partners will co-lead, with the involvement of the <a href="https://esciencelab.org.uk/projects/eosc-entrust/">University of Manchester</a>, the work to demonstrate that existing federated analytics tools can be packaged within the <a href="https://w3id.org/5s-crate/">Five Safes RO-Crate</a> profile.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="ensuring-adoption-longevity-and-scalability">Ensuring adoption, longevity and scalability</h3>

<p>Beyond technical advancements, EOSC-ENTRUST will engage in targeted outreach efforts to expand the provider network and develop policy recommendations, ensuring adoption, longevity and scalability.&nbsp;</p>

<p>In this crucial element, HDR UK partners are co-leading in the policy and dissemination activities, with the involvement of <a href="https://www.dundee.ac.uk/">University of Dundee</a> partners in establishing a TRE Provider forum for sharing know-how.&nbsp;</p>
