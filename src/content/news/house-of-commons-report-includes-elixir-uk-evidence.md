<p>In 2021, ELIXIR-UK contributed to the inquiry from the <a href="https://committees.parliament.uk/committee/135/science-and-technology-committee">Science, Innovation and Technology Committee</a> of the House of Commons, which allowed our members to come together and provide evidence gathered from our own work on the issues of reproducibility and research integrity.</p>

<p>In submitting our <a href="https://committees.parliament.uk/writtenevidence/39723/pdf/">written evidence</a> in September 2021, we highlighted three main threads:</p>

<ul>
<li>Reproducibility is a complex topic and rather than focusing on a narrow view of reproducibility of scientific results, it is more helpful to focus on <em>transparency</em> in the research process, and the <em>reusability</em> of results as both are key to generating research that will be reproducible.</li>

<li>Institutions must ensure career paths for data stewards and software engineers were formally recognised and supported. Our <a href="https://fellowship.elixiruknode.org/">Data Steward Training Fellowship</a> is an excellent example of embedding data stewards and their Research Data Management know-how into universities and research institutes.</li>

<li>UKRI councils should require the inclusion of a costed activity related to research transparency in funding requests, with a research transparency plan extending the current data management plans, and investigators held to account that they deliver on the plan.</li>
</ul>

<p>The importance of open data (specifically in the life sciences) and the role it plays in driving innovation was highlighted previously in <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/news/new-report-shows-open-data-heart-innovation">the Open Data ELIXIR Europe report</a>. </p>

<p>We are excited to see the above threads taken into the <a href="https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/cm5803/cmselect/cmsctech/101/summary.html">Reproducibility and Research Integrity report</a>, released by the Committee on 10 May 2023, alongside many other great evidence-based suggestions from colleagues. <a href="https://committees.parliament.uk/committee/135/science-innovation-and-technology-committee/news/195158/mps-call-for-research-sector-reforms-to-address-concerns-with-reproducibility-of-science/">The key recommendations from the report can be found here</a>.</p>
