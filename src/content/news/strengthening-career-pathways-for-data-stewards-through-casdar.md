<p>On <strong>18 September 2025</strong>, the UK’s <strong>Research Data Stewardship</strong> community gathered for the launch of the <strong><a href="https://casdar.ac.uk/">Careers and Skills for Data-driven Research (CaSDaR) Network</a></strong>, a <strong>UKRI-funded Network+ project</strong> dedicated to professionalising the role of <strong>Data Stewards</strong>. The hybrid event, held at the <strong>Library of Birmingham</strong> and online, showcased presentations and active participation from diverse <strong>ELIXIR-UK members</strong>.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="bringing-bioscience-expertise-to-national-discussions">Bringing bioscience expertise to national discussions</h4>

<p>Research Data Management (<strong>RDM</strong>) in biosciences is among the most advanced, providing insights that can guide other sectors. Representing <strong>ELIXIR-UK</strong>, <strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/robert-andrews/" data-type="people" data-id="18733">Robert Andrews</a></strong> contributed to the opening panel, advising the CaSDaR coordination team on building national data stewardship capacity. <strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/xenia-perez-sitja/" data-type="people" data-id="18755">Xenia Perez Sitja</a></strong> shared ELIXIR-UK’s experience in establishing local <strong>Data Steward networks</strong> and <strong>training initiatives</strong>, demonstrating how frameworks, competence models, and handbooks from <strong>ELIXIR-UK</strong> and <strong>ELIXIR Europe</strong> can inform UK-wide strategies. Both are members of the <strong>CaSDaR Managing Board</strong> and will continue to guide the project’s development while representing ELIXIR-UK members.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="collaboration-across-the-uk-data-community">Collaboration across the UK data community</h4>

<p>The programme featured insights from multiple UK organisations, including many that are ELIXIR-UK members: <strong>UKCEH, UCL, the University of Manchester, and the OLS/Turing Institute</strong>. The event highlighted the importance of <strong>professional recognition</strong>, sustainable <strong>career pathways for Data Stewards</strong> and collaborative knowledge sharing. CaSDaR also launched its first <strong>funding call</strong> to support new <strong>data stewardship activities</strong>.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="elixir-uk-s-ongoing-commitment">ELIXIR-UK’s ongoing commitment</h4>

<p>ELIXIR-UK continues to drive the <strong>professionalisation of research data roles</strong>, supporting career pathways, training and <strong>FAIR data practices</strong> across UK institutions. By contributing to initiatives like CaSDaR, ELIXIR-UK strengthens the UK’s <strong>research data ecosystem</strong> and ensures that the expertise of <strong>technical professionals</strong> is recognised as central to high-quality, sustainable science.</p>
