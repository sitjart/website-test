<p>We are really excited to announce that <a href="https://openlifesci.org/">Open Life Science</a> (OLS) is the newest member of ELIXIR-UK, bringing us to 24 members!</p>

<p><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/about/members/">Check out our other members here.</a></p>

<p>This mentoring and training programme for those interested in becoming Open Science ambassadors is a fantastic way for individuals to take open science principles they have learnt forward into their own work, and out into their communities.</p>

<p><a href="https://yo-yehudi.com/">Yo Yehudi</a>, Executive Director and Co-Founder of OLS will be joining the ELIXIR-UK Steering Committee as the OLS representative. They are currently also studying for an EngD degree at the University of Manchester with a focus on human aspects of software from a life science perspective.</p>

<p>As a member, OLS will be part of discussions around ELIXIR-UK resources and have influence in this international community.</p>

<p>If you belong to the OLS Community and would like to learn more about how you can get involved in ELIXIR now that you are also a member, you can <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/about/contact/">sign up to our mailing list</a>, see which <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/focus-groups">focus groups</a> and <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/communities">communities</a> you can get involved in, or contact us directly for more information.</p>

<p>If you belong to an organisation which is not a member of ELIXIR-UK but you would like to be more involved in ELIXIR, you can find out more about the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/about/new-members/">application process</a>, <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/about/contact/">sign up for our newsletter</a> to see how you could benefit, or get in touch with us with any questions.</p>
