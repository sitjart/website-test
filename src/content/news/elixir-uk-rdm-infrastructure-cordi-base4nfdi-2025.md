<p>From 26–29 August 2025, <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/carole-goble/" data-type="people" data-id="18640">Carole Goble</a>, Joint Head of Node at ELIXIR-UK, participated in two major research data events in Aachen, Germany: the Conference on Research Data Infrastructure (<a href="https://www.nfdi.de/cordi-2025/?lang=en">CoRDI 2025</a>) and the 2nd <a href="https://base4nfdi.de/news-events/events/user-conference-2025">Base4NFDI User Conference</a>.</p>

<p>At CoRDI 2025, Carole chaired the track on RDM Infrastructures, which featured ELIXIR-UK-related contributions such as:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Two posters on ELIXIR-UK endorsed services: “mTeSS-X: A federated, FAIR-aligned platform for distributed management and exchange of training resources” and ”Flexible Metadata Structuring for Research Data Management through FAIRDOM-SEEK: Implementing tailored and complex metadata schemes in SEEK”</li>

<li>The track highlighted workflows and reproducibility, reflecting ELIXIR-UK’s work, and particularly the University of Manchester, on services including <a href="https://www.researchobject.org/ro-crate/">RO-Crate</a> and <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/workflowhub/" data-type="service" data-id="18745">WorkflowHub</a>.</li>
</ul>

<p>At Base4NFDI, Carole participated in the panel discussion “How to build sustainable infrastructures – lessons learnt and future perspectives”, engaging with international experts on technical and organisational approaches to research infrastructure sustainability.</p>

<p>These engagements underscore ELIXIR-UK’s commitment to shaping sustainable, FAIR-aligned research data infrastructure, connecting UK expertise with international stakeholders.</p>
