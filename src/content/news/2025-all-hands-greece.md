<p>The <strong>ELIXIR All Hands Meeting 2025</strong>, held in Thessaloniki, Greece, 2-6 June, brought together members from across Europe to exchange ideas, coordinate infrastructure development, and shape the shared future of life science data. Among the many contributors, colleagues from UK-based institutes participated actively across sessions, working alongside partners from other Nodes to strengthen collaborations and advance areas of shared interest.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="working-together-on-fair-practices-training-and-community-building"><strong>Working together on FAIR practices, training and community building</strong></h4>

<p>More than 25 participants from the UK Node attended the meeting, alongside colleagues from <strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/collaborator/biofair/" data-type="collaborator" data-id="22923">BioFAIR</a></strong>. Together, they represented a wide range of expertise across the ELIXIR community, contributing to six co-led sessions, panel discussions, and 22 UK-related posters, as well as broad engagement in both technical and strategic conversations.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Workshops on enhancing RDM resources (<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/munazah-andrabi/" data-type="people" data-id="18649"><strong>Munazah Andrabi</strong>)</a>, empowering knowledge sharing (<strong>Xenia Perez-Sitja and Munazah Andrabi</strong>), and mentorship programmes for female leaders in ELIXIR&nbsp; (<strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/xenia-perez-sitja/" data-type="people" data-id="18755">Xenia Perez-Sitja</a></strong>) demonstrated our commitment to building inclusive and collaborative FAIR ecosystems.</p>

<p>A workshop on <em>Connecting ELIXIR Communities</em> featured <strong>Tony Burdett </strong>in a coordination role, helping build synergies across ELIXIR.</p>

<p>These sessions highlighted shared efforts to build inclusive communities across Nodes, where training, equity and infrastructure are not separate topics but interlinked ones. They also reflected the value of cross-Node expertise in tackling complex challenges – from ontologies to phenotype harmonisation – and building tools that work across disciplines.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="closing-keynote-led-by-an-elixir-uk-member"><strong>Closing keynote led by an ELIXIR-UK member</strong></h4>

<p>The meeting concluded with a keynote by <strong>Irene Papatheodorou</strong>, “<em>Decoding phenotypes at the cell type level”. </em>Through her research in computational single-cell genomics, her talk inspired a vision for how the ELIXIR community can facilitate more coordinated, infrastructure-supported approaches to understanding cellular phenotypes, with collaboration at the heart of that effort.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="collaboration-as-a-foundation-for-sustainability"><strong>Collaboration as a foundation for sustainability</strong></h4>

<p>Throughout the meeting, strategic discussions around <strong>EOSC</strong>, service sustainability, and long-term infrastructure alignment were shaped by contributors from multiple Nodes. In a plenary session, <strong>Munazah Andrabi</strong> emphasised the importance of non-hierarchical, community-led collaboration across Nodes, drawing on the example of the <strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/rdmkit/" data-type="service" data-id="18748">RDMkit</a></strong> – a successful resource built through equal participation, trust and transparency that is now a recommended resource by the European Commission.&nbsp;</p>

<p>The meeting reinforced that research infrastructure is not just built from services and data, but through relationships, shared priorities and the willingness to work across institutional and national borders.&nbsp;</p>

<p>Our participation reflected not only areas of national strength but, more importantly, the value of sustained collaboration across Nodes.&nbsp;</p>
