<p>UKRI is in the process of updating and harmonising its research data expectations across all councils, following the publication of the draft <a href="https://engagementhub.ukri.org/ukri-openresearch/developing-ukris-research-data-policy/" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow"><strong>UKRI-wide Research Data Policy in April 2025</strong></a>. While applicants and award holders must continue following existing council-specific policies (such as the BBSRC Data Sharing Policy and the UKRI Common Principles), the draft policy signals upcoming shifts in how research data, software, and other digital objects arising from UKRI funding should be managed and shared.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h3>

<p>In this session, <strong>Dr Richard Brown</strong> will:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Outline the current UKRI Research Data Policy landscape</li>

<li>Discuss the draft harmonised policy and expected <strong>timelines</strong></li>

<li>Highlight what these changes mean specifically for the <strong>biosciences</strong></li>

<li>Invite <strong>feedback from the community</strong></li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-attend"><strong>Who should attend</strong></h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Research data managers and data stewards</li>

<li>Researchers handling data in UKRI-funded projects</li>

<li>Project managers and research support staff</li>

<li>Policy and open research professionals</li>

<li>Anyone interested in the future direction of UKRI data management expectations</li>
</ul>
