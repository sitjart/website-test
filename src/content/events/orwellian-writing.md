<p>This Training Club session explores practical writing techniques for creating clear, effective teaching materials. The session draws on guidance from George Orwell’s essay <em>Politics and the English Language</em>, adapting his principles for use in educational and training contexts.</p>

<p>Through a short presentation and interactive activities, we will translate Orwell’s rules into practical strategies for teaching-focused writing.</p>

<p>The session will cover:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>The use of metaphors – when they help, when they hinder, and how to create new ones</li>

<li>The benefits and limits of simple language in teaching materials</li>

<li>Writing with clarity while being kind to yourself as an author</li>
</ul>
