<h3>ELIXIR-UK Human Data Community Call with Sven Twardziok</h3>
Join us for the next ELIXIR-UK Human Data Community call, where we are excited to welcome Sven Twardziok, Group Leader of Data Management at Berlin Institute of Health at Charité. Sven will present recent developments around the Workflow Execution Service (WES) and WESkit, providing insights into their implementation and how they integrate with other GA4GH standards, particularly the Task Execution Service (TES).

Sven's presentation will be followed by a <strong>Q&amp;A session, </strong>during which attendees can ask questions and discuss potential applications of WES and WESkit within Trusted Research Environments (TREs).
<h4><strong>Speaker</strong></h4>
<strong>Sven O. Twardziok</strong> is the Group Leader of Data Management at Berlin Institute of Health at Charité.
<h4><strong>Who should attend</strong></h4>
This event is ideal for TRE providers, researchers, data managers, and anyone interested in the use of GA4GH standards, particularly WES and TES, in the management and execution of workflows.
