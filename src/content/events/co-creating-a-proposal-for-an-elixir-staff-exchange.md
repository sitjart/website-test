<p>ELIXIR Europe is due to reopen its <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/internal-projects/commissioned-services/capacity-building-knowledge-exchange" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">staff exchanges</a> to support skills development, collaboration and knowledge transfer across Nodes. This Training Club session will bring the UK community together to shape a proposal that reflects our training needs and areas of expertise.<br>We will work through the following:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Identify gaps and priorities in UK training capability</li>

<li>Shortlist the top three topics that would benefit most from a staff exchange</li>

<li>Discuss where incoming expertise from other Nodes would be valuable, and what expertise the UK could offer in return</li>

<li>Discuss which communities/platforms/focus groups would align with our proposal</li>

<li>Draft the outline of a UK proposal to submit to ELIXIR Europe</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="why-this-matters"><strong>Why this matters</strong></h3>

<p>Attending this session gives you the chance to directly influence the support the UK could receive. A successful proposal may create opportunities for travel, knowledge exchange and targeted skill development that address real needs identified by our community.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-attend"><strong>Who should attend</strong></h3>

<p>Anyone involved in training design, delivery or coordination across ELIXIR-UK.</p>
