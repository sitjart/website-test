<p class="has-large-font-size">It’s February, it’s Valentine’s – we talk about what we love about Data Stewardship.</p>

<p>During this month’s Elevenses RDM Club Call, we adopt a relaxed format and panel. FAIR data stewards in the UK will talk about how they came into their roles and signpost the data stewardship resources they love using in their jobs. </p>

<p>Join us to hear the stories of Angus Whyte (DCC), Liz Merrifield (Cardiff University), Munazah Andrabi (University of Manchester) and Nick Owen (UCL).</p>

<p>Listen, ask questions to the panel,  share your data management experiences and contribute to a shared national document cataloguing useful data stewardship resources.</p>
