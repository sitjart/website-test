<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Join us for three days of creativity, innovation and collaboration at the HDR UK &amp; ELIXIR-UK Joint Hackathon. We are bringing together UK professionals in health data and life sciences to tackle shared technical challenges across the ecosystem.</span></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Do you have an idea that needs a little push? Suggest your project for a chance to engage in intensive brainstorming, hands-on coding and true networking.</span> Or come join us to accelerate one of the proposed projects –&nbsp;there's something for everyone.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect"><b>What to expect</b></h3>

<p><b>Scope</b><b><br></b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Engage in action-packed sessions where your ideas can come to life, or be part of a team that makes it possible. Projects should align with </span><a href="https://www.hdruk.ac.uk/about-us/our-strategy/"><span style="font-weight: 400;">HDR UK</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and/or </span><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/what-we-do/" rel=""><span style="font-weight: 400;">ELIXIR-UK</span></a><span style="font-weight: 400;">'s missions and technical priorities.</span></p>

<p><b>Goals</b></p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><b>Innovate together:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> advance the development of an open-source infrastructure for data integration and analysis.</span></li>

<li><b>Collaborate and connect:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> bring together technical professionals in health informatics and bioinformatics to work on common interests.</span></li>

<li><b>Strengthen bonds:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> increase collaboration between&nbsp; ELIXIR-UK and HDR UK technologists to tackle shared challenges.&nbsp;</span></li>

<li><b>Create impact:</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> form lasting collaborations that have the potential to deliver solutions to accelerate scientific discoveries.</span></li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-attend"><b>Who should attend</b></h3>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">The Hackathon is designed for a diverse range of participants, including but not limited to:</span></p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><b>Health data and life science researchers and technologists</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: engage with peers, share your expertise, and collaborate on your projects or those of others.</span></li>

<li><b>Non-technical roles</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: strategic thinkers, engagement specialists, trainers, coordinators, and other technical non-coding professionals are essential to driving projects forward.</span></li>

<li><b>Early-career researchers</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">: join as an opportunity to gain hands-on experience, build your network, and contribute to significant projects.</span></li>
</ul>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Everyone passionate about advancing health data and life sciences is welcome, regardless of technical background. Bring your unique skills and perspectives to help shape diverse solutions to varied challenges</span></p>
</div></div>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="project-submissions"><b>Project submissions</b></h3>

<p>Project submissions are now closed, and the 2025 projects have been selected. </p>

<div class="wp-block-buttons">
<div class="wp-block-button"><a class="wp-block-button__link wp-element-button" href="https://github.com/HDRUK/hackathon-projects-2025">Check the projects</a></div>
</div>

<p></p>

<p><span style="font-weight: 400;">Each proposal should have </span><b>at least two leads</b><span style="font-weight: 400;"> and include:</span></p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">Names, affiliations, and email addresses of the leads</span></li>

<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">A project description (max. 500 words)</span></li>

<li><span style="font-weight: 400;">In the description, please include how the project aligns with the mission and technical priorities of HDR UK and/or ELIXIR-UK</span></li>
