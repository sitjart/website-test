<p>The FAIR-in-action Bridge is a BioFAIR Pathfinder project led by the ELIXIR-UK RDM Club Coordinators to co-create two facilitator-ready playbooks: How to approach FAIRification and How to communicate and advocate FAIR.</p>

<p><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/project/fair-in-action-bridge/" data-type="project" data-id="25067">Find out more about the project.</a></p>

<p>These playbooks will translate existing FAIR guidance into practical, reusable activities, templates and examples that RDM professionals can adapt and deploy locally.</p>

<p class="has-medium-font-size">This first contentathon is a scoping session.</p>

<p>We want your voice in defining what each playbook should contain. We'll be asking:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>What does each playbook need to cover?</li>

<li>Where are the current gaps between FAIR guidance and day-to-day practice?</li>

<li>What structure would make these playbooks easy to pick up and adapt?</li>

<li>Which existing resources do you have that should be embedded?</li>
</ul>

Whether you have five minutes of opinions or two hours of ideas, we'll be glad to have you.</p>
