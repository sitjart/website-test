<p>AI assets – including <strong>models and datasets</strong> – are increasingly fundamental research outputs in need of good data management practices to remain <strong>reusable, reproducible and sustainable</strong> over time.</p>

<p>In this RDM Club Call, <strong><a href="https://www.linkedin.com/in/gavin-farrell97/">Gavin Farrell </a>(ELIXIR Ireland &amp; ELIXIR Italy)</strong>, a contributor to <a href="https://elixir-europe.org/focus-groups/ai-ecosystem">ELIXIR’s <strong>AI Ecosystem</strong> Focus Group work</a>, will introduce the concept of <strong>Open and Sustainable AI (OSAI)</strong>. Grounded in the ELIXIR Europe Machine Learning Focus Group work that culminated in the 2025 preprint <em><a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16619">Open and Sustainable AI: challenges, opportunities and the road ahead in the life sciences</a></em>, the talk presents <strong>nine OSAI guidelines</strong> for ensuring AI datasets and models can be reused and reproduced while also supporting <strong>environmental sustainability</strong>.</p>

<p>The session will also highlight a <strong>living landscape of <a href="https://osai.dome-ml.org/ai-ecosystem">300+ supports</a></strong> available for community application, designed to help implement OSAI guidelines in practice.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="speaker">Speaker</h3>

<p><strong>Gavin Farrell</strong> – <em>ELIXIR Ireland &amp; ELIXIR Italy, contributor to ELIXIR’s AI Ecosystem focus group</em></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-attend">Who should attend</h3>

<p>This session is designed for professionals across the research, including but not limited to:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Research Data Managers and Research Software Quality Managers:</strong>&nbsp;those looking to standardise AI workflows and integrate asset management into institutional/network-wide infrastructure.</li>

<li><strong>AI Experimenters and Researchers:</strong>&nbsp;individuals who need practical frameworks to make their AI code and datasets more reusable, reproducible, sustainable, and open.</li>

<li><strong>Data Stewards:</strong>&nbsp;professionals interested in applying community vetted guidelines and contributing new domain-specific implementations to the wider ecosystem.</li>
</ul>
