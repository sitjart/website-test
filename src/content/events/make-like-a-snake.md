<p>The ability to orchestrate a complex analysis of a large dataset and to make that analysis both scalable and repeatable is a challenge that any modern bioinformatician must tackle, especially where high-throughput sequencing is involved. The <strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/snakemake/" data-type="service" data-id="23352">Snakemake</a> workflow </strong>engine is a powerful tool that can help with this, but many users at first struggle to understand what Snakemake can do for them and how to begin working with it.</p>

<p>Effective use of Snakemake requires grasping both the core concepts of the system itself and the ecosystem of software and practices that are used with it. This session will outline a structured programme of Snakemake training and how it fits into the broader bioinformatics training landscape. Additionally, we will discuss ongoing work with <strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/collaborator/biofair/" data-type="collaborator" data-id="22923">BioFAIR</a></strong> and <strong><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/workflowhub/" data-type="service" data-id="18745">WorkflowHub</a></strong>, which is helping to connect these projects with the Snakemake community. The session will also introduce new teaching materials designed to guide Snakemake learners toward the resources that ultimately enable <strong>reproducible research</strong>.</p>

<p><strong>Key Topics:</strong></p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Understanding Snakemake and its capabilities</li>

<li>Building scalable and reproducible bioinformatics workflows</li>

<li>Training resources and their role in bioinformatics education</li>

<li>Integrating Snakemake with <strong>BioFAIR</strong> and <strong>WorkflowHub</strong></li>

<li>New teaching materials for Snakemake learners</li>
</ul>

<p>Join this UK Training Club Call to learn how Snakemake can transform your approach to bioinformatics analysis and workflow automation!</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="speaker"><strong>Speaker</strong></h3>

<p><strong>Timothy Booth</strong>, Bioinformatician &amp; Software Developer, Edinburgh Genomics</p>

<p>Timothy is a bioinformatician and software developer at <strong>Edinburgh Genomics. </strong>At this core sequencing facility, he has developed multiple pipelines for rapid and reliable sequencing data analysis, with <strong>Snakemake</strong> at the heart of these workflows. He has contributed to both the <strong>Snakemake developer community</strong> and the <strong>Snakemake codebase</strong> itself.</p>

<p>With over <strong>two decades of teaching experience</strong> in <strong>Linux skills, programming, and data analysis</strong>, Timothy has designed an <strong>introductory Snakemake course</strong> that is now part of the <strong>Software Carpentries extended curriculum</strong>. This course has become one of the most popular training offerings from <strong>Edinburgh Genomics</strong>, attracting participants from around the world.</p>
