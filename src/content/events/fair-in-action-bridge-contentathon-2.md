<p>On 28 May, we're rolling up our sleeves to draft the first FAIR-in-action playbook: How to approach FAIRification.</p>

<p>This is the practical core of the RDM Club's BioFAIR pathfinder project – turning guidance from RDMkit, the FAIR Cookbook, FAIRsharing, WorkflowHub and Galaxy into ready-to-deliver workshop activities, templates, slide decks and facilitator scripts that RDM professionals can take to their own institutions.</p>

<p><a href="https://elixiruknode.org/project/fair-in-action-bridge/">Find out more about the project</a></p>

<p>The day is structured into focused sections (full schedule shared soon) so contributors can drop into the parts that match their interests and expertise – for example, FAIRification of specific data types (e.g., imaging, NGS), template design, facilitator scripts, or activity instructions.</p>

<p class="has-medium-font-size">You don't need to commit to the whole day.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-you-ll-do">What you'll do</h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Draft activities, templates and scripts in small breakout groups with acknowledgement of contributions in an open repository. </li>

<li>Map content to existing FAIR resources</li>

<li>Test ideas against your own institutional context</li>

<li>Help shape Playbook v1, which will then be tested across six UK RPOs</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="main-audience">Main audience</h3>

<p>Data stewards, data managers, RDM leads, trainers, research technical professionals</p>
