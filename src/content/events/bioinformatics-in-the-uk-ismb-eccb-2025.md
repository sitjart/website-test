<p>Join us for a special one-day UK track at ISMB / ECCB 2025, showcasing the best of British bioinformatics research, innovation, and talent. This track is part of the 33rd Conference on Intelligent Systems for Molecular Biology and the 24th European Conference on Computational Biology (ISMB/ECCB 2025), the world's largest bioinformatics and computational biology conference.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="focus-areas">Focus areas</h3>

<ol class="wp-block-list">
<li>Biodiversity &amp; Agritech</li>

<li>Health</li>

<li>AI in bioinformatics</li>
</ol>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="programme-features">Programme features</h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Invited keynotes from academia</li>

<li>Including an industry panel</li>

<li>UK focus: offering a central space for national initiatives</li>

<li>Evening poster reception for socialising and networking</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="support-for-early-career-researchers-1">Support for Early Career Researchers</h3>

<p>We are committed to supporting the next generation of bioinformatics leaders. Information on the application process for these bursaries will be shared following abstract acceptance.</p>
