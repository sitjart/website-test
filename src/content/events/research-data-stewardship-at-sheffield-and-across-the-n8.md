<p>The University of Sheffield is strengthening data stewardship across its research community through coordinated initiatives that improve visibility, recognition, and support for data professionals. Current activities include a Library-based <a href="https://osf.io/preprints/metaarxiv/2ev6k_v1">FAIR data stewardship service pilot</a>, mapping stewardship practices across departments, and developing evidence to support a sustainable, university-wide approach.</p>

<p>These efforts have led to the creation of the <a href="https://sheffield.ac.uk/library/research-data-management/data-steward-network">Sheffield Data Stewards Network</a> and the establishment of a new <a href="https://n8cir.org.uk/our-networks/data-stewards-network/">N8 Data Stewards Network</a>, connecting regional institutions and contributing to the <a href="https://www.rd-alliance.org/groups/data-steward-career-tracks-wg/activity/">Research Data Alliance’s Data Steward Career Tracks working group</a>.</p>

<p>This session will share lessons learned, challenges encountered and future directions for data stewardship at Sheffield and across the <a href="https://www.n8research.org.uk/">N8 Research Partnership</a>.</p>
