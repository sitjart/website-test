<p>Instead, join us for a relaxed session with the community co-leads, Saskia and Tim, to:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Catch up on current community activities</li>

<li>Ask questions or raise discussion points</li>

<li>Share ideas and feedback about our UK Community or the wider ELIXIR human data landscape</li>
</ul>

<p>Whether you're actively involved or simply curious, we welcome you to drop in and say hello.</p>
