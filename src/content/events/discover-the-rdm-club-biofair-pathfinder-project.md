<p>The RDM Club is excited to share news about our Club's <strong>BioFAIR Pathfinder project</strong> – and what it means for our community.</p>

<p>This community call will explore how this BioFAIR Pathfinder project is opening up new opportunities for FAIR implementation, including hands-on playbooks and participation in FAIR implementation events.</p>

<p>Whether you're training researchers or advocating for FAIR data adoption at an institutional level, this session will give you the tools and context to get involved.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h3>

<p>A 1-hour session with a presentation followed by a Q&amp;A. This presentation will be recorded.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-join">Who should join</h3>

<p>This session is particularly relevant for:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Anyone training researchers in FAIR data principles</li>

<li>People advocating for FAIR implementation at an institutional level</li>

<li>Research data managers looking for practical, hands-on resources</li>

<li>Anyone interested in getting involved in a BioFAIR Pathfinder project</li>
</ul>
