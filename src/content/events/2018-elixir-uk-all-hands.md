<p>The annual ELIXIR-UK All Hands brings together people working at ELIXIR-UK consortium member institutions for this two-day meeting, which for 2018 is hosted by Birmingham University.</p>

<h3 id="what-is-the-workshop-about" class="wp-block-heading">What is the workshop about?</h3>

<p>The annual ELIXIR-UK All Hands brings together people working at ELIXIR-UK consortium member institutions to:</p>

<ol>
<li>Share knowledge and experience from the previous year of ELIXIR activities</li>

<li>Network with other people working on life science data infrastructure across the UK</li>

<li>Work together to develop a strategy for the future of ELIXIR-UK</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="target-audience" class="wp-block-heading">Target audience</h3>

<p>All members of ELIXIR-UK consortium member institutions working on ELIXIR-related projects are welcome to join the two-day meeting, which this year is hosted by Birmingham University.</p>
