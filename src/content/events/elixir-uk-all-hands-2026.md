<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="about-the-elixir-uk-all-hands-meeting">About the ELIXIR-UK All Hands meeting</h2>

<p>The ELIXIR-UK All Hands is the UK Node's flagship community event, held annually to bring together members, collaborators, and partners from across the UK's life sciences research infrastructure. It is open to all members and collaborators of the ELIXIR-UK Node, spanning research data management, training, human data, bioinformatics tools, digital research infrastructures and much more.</p>

<p>Previous meetings have taken place in <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/event/elixir-uk-all-hands-2025/" data-type="event" data-id="23736">Exeter (2025)</a> and have featured sessions on FAIR data, open science policy, flexible funding calls, AI in the life sciences, and community-led world cafés.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h3>

<p>While the full programme is still in development, past All Hands meetings have included:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Keynote and community sessions</strong>&nbsp;from ELIXIR-UK leadership, host institutions and member organisations</li>

<li><strong>Funding and policy updates</strong>, including BBSRC, MRC and BioFAIR updates</li>

<li><strong>Flash talks and poster presentations</strong>&nbsp;from members and early-career researchers</li>

<li><strong>World café discussions</strong>&nbsp;to shape ELIXIR-UK priorities on topics like FAIR data, AI strategy, sensitive human data and training</li>

<li><strong>Networking and social programme,</strong>&nbsp;including dinner on the night of the first day. </li>
</ul>

<p>We especially welcome contributions from early-career researchers and those new to the ELIXIR-UK community (including prospective members). </p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-attend">Who should attend</h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Staff and researchers at ELIXIR-UK member organisations</li>

<li>Bioinformaticians, data stewards and research software engineers in member organisations (including prospective ones) or collaborators.</li>

<li>Policy, communications and project management professionals in research infrastructure.</li>

<li>ELIXIR Europe international partners and invited collaborators.</li>

<li>Early-career researchers interested in research data management, bioinformatics and open science.</li>
</ul>

<div class="wp-block-greenshift-blocks-accordionitem gs-accordion-item gspb_accordionitem-gsbp-4f903cd gsclose" id="gspb_accordionitem-gsbp-4f903cd" itemscope itemprop="mainEntity" itemtype="https://schema.org/Question"><div class="gs-accordion-item__title" aria-expanded="false" role="button" tabindex="0" aria-controls="gspb-accordion-item-content-gsbp-4f903cd"><div class="gs-accordion-item__heading">What is the ELIXIR-UK All Hands Meeting?</div><meta itemprop="name" content="What is the ELIXIR-UK All Hands Meeting?"/><span class="iconfortoggle"><span class="gs-iconbefore"></span><span class="gs-iconafter"></span></span></div><div class="gs-accordion-item__content" itemscope itemprop="acceptedAnswer" itemtype="https://schema.org/Answer" id="gspb-accordion-item-content-gsbp-4f903cd"><div class="gs-accordion-item__text" itemprop="text">
<p>The ELIXIR-UK All Hands is the annual national gathering of the UK Node of ELIXIR, the European infrastructure for life science data. It brings together researchers, data managers, trainers and infrastructure professionals to review progress, discuss priorities and future direction and build collaborations.</p>
</div></div></div>

<div class="wp-block-greenshift-blocks-accordionitem gs-accordion-item gspb_accordionitem-gsbp-7d7f7f9 gsclose" id="gspb_accordionitem-gsbp-7d7f7f9" itemscope itemprop="mainEntity" itemtype="https://schema.org/Question"><div class="gs-accordion-item__title" aria-expanded="false" role="button" tabindex="0" aria-controls="gspb-accordion-item-content-gsbp-7d7f7f9"><div class="gs-accordion-item__heading">Who can attend the ELIXIR-UK All Hands 2026?</div><meta itemprop="name" content="Who can attend the ELIXIR-UK All Hands 2026?"/><span class="iconfortoggle"><span class="gs-iconbefore"></span><span class="gs-iconafter"></span></span></div><div class="gs-accordion-item__content" itemscope itemprop="acceptedAnswer" itemtype="https://schema.org/Answer" id="gspb-accordion-item-content-gsbp-7d7f7f9"><div class="gs-accordion-item__text" itemprop="text">
<p>The event is open to all <a href="/members">members</a> and collaborators of the ELIXIR-UK Node. Priority for places is typically given to participants from ELIXIR-UK member organisations, but we are open to all, including collaborators and prospective members. </p>
</div></div></div>
