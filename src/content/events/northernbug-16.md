<p>NorthernBUG 16 brings the network's regular meeting to the University of Bradford for a day of talks, posters and open discussion. The event is free and open to anyone working with computational tools in the life sciences.</p>

<p>The morning includes an ELIXIR-UK services and activities session (10:35–12:00), followed by two rounds of short talks covering topics from multiomic data integration and spatial transcriptomics to AI-assisted single-cell analysis, alongside a poster session and flash talks. The day closes with prizes and a trip to Wallers Brewery.</p>

<p><strong>Funder acknowledgement</strong><br>Supported and sponsored by ELIXIR-UK. Travel scholarships for this meeting were funded by the UKRI Digital Research Skills Catalyst.</p>
