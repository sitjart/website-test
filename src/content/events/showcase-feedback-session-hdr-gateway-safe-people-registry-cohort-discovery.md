<p>Join the February Human Data community call for a live, interactive showcase of three key technologies developed by HDR UK that support access, discovery and trust in the landscape of human data research:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>HDR Gateway</li>

<li>Safe People Registry</li>

<li>Cohort Discovery</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h3>

<p>This session will include short demonstrations of each technology, highlighting how they work in practice, recent developments and their future direction.</p>

<p>The focus, however, is not just on showcasing tools – it’s on learning from you.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="why-attend">Why attend</h3>

<p>This is a highly participatory session designed to gather feedback from people who work with human data in real research settings. We’re keen to hear your perspectives on:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>What works well and what doesn’t</li>

<li>How these tools fit into your workflows</li>

<li>Gaps, priorities, and opportunities for improvement</li>

<li>Ideas that could shape the next phase of open development</li>
</ul>

<p>Your insights will directly inform how the technology evolves, helping ensure it continues to meet the needs of the broader human data research community.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-join">Who should join</h3>

<p>This session is particularly relevant for:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Researchers and data scientists working with human data</li>

<li>Data stewards, data access committees and governance professionals</li>

<li>Infrastructure developers and service providers</li>

<li>Anyone interested in trusted access and safe reuse of sensitive data</li>
</ul>
