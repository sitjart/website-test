<p class="has-large-font-size">Requirements for FAIR metadata, repositories and discovery</p>

<p>Phenotype definitions remain difficult to discover, compare and reuse across fragmented resources. A staged consensus-building exercise identified community requirements for metadata, repository functionality and coordinated discovery.</p>

<p>The findings are informing international work to align phenotype repositories and illustrate why technical standards must be developed, implemented and governed with continued user involvement.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="speaker">Speaker</h2>

<p><strong>Jackie MacArthur</strong><br><em>Senior Scientific Programme Manager</em>, BHF Data Science Centre</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect </h2>

<p>The session will examine community requirements for FAIR metadata, repository functionality and coordinated discovery, and discuss how these findings are informing international efforts to align phenotype repositories. It will also highlight the importance of involving users throughout the development, implementation and governance of technical standards to ensure that infrastructure meets real-world research needs.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-is-this-for">Who is this for</h2>

<p>Whether you work with phenotype data, develop research infrastructure, or are interested in FAIR data practices, this session will provide valuable insights into building sustainable, community-driven phenomics infrastructure.</p>
