<p>This UK Human Data &amp; Tech Club community call will introduce two platforms for genetic scores – <strong>PGS Catalog</strong> and <strong>OmicsPred</strong> – and the standards and infrastructure that keep them useful across research and clinical settings.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="speaker">Speaker</h3>

<p><strong>Laurent Gil </strong><br>Principal Software Developer, <em>Wellcome Sanger Institute </em><br></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h3>

<p>Genetic scores summarise an individual's genetic predisposition to a trait or disease, and their value depends on being findable, comparable and reproducible across studies.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>What genetic scores are and how they're used in research</li>

<li>The standardised metadata and file format that keep scores comparable across studies and platforms</li>

<li>The infrastructure and user interfaces behind PGS Catalog and OmicsPred</li>

<li>A software developer's role in building and maintaining both projects</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-is-this-for">Who is this for</h3>

<p>This session is particularly relevant if you:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Work with genetic or polygenic scores in your research</li>

<li>Build or maintain research data platforms</li>

<li>Are interested in metadata and file format standardisation</li>

<li>Develop cloud infrastructure – websites, REST APIs – for research tools</li>
</ul>
