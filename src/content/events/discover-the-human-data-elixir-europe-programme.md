<p>We are excited to invite you to the upcoming UK Human Data Community Call. This session is a great opportunity to learn more about the ELIXIR Human Data Communities at the European level, their activities and how you can get involved.</p>

<p>Our featured speaker, <strong>Magda Chegkazi</strong>, <strong>Programme Manager for Human Genomics and Translational Data</strong>, will lead a<strong> </strong>20-minute presentation exploring the work of the ELIXIR Human Data Communities. <strong>Magda</strong> will share insights into current activities, highlight opportunities for collaboration and provide practical guidance for those looking to join or engage with these communities.</p>

<p>Whether you’re new to ELIXIR or already familiar, this session will demystify the role of ELIXIR communities and outline clear pathways for participation. Following <strong>Magda</strong>'s presentation, the second half of the meeting will open up for <strong>questions, discussion and updates</strong> – an opportunity to share your thoughts, clarify doubts and connect with others in the human data field.</p>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-attend"><strong>Who should attend?</strong></h4>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Researchers and professionals interested in <strong>ELIXIR human data activities</strong></li>

<li>Those looking to understand how to get involved with ELIXIR Communities</li>

<li>Anyone in the UK working with human data</li>
</ul>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h4 class="wp-block-heading" id="why-attend"><strong>Why attend?</strong></h4>

<p>Based on experience from the <strong>ELIXIR-UK All Hands</strong>, there is increasing interest in the human data space to engage with ELIXIR, but uncertainty on how to start. This call will provide clarity on:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>The role of ELIXIR Communities</li>

<li>Opportunities for participation</li>

<li>Practical guidance for entry and collaboration</li>
</ul>
