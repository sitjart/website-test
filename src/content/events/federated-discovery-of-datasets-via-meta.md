<p>In this month's RDM Club meeting,  we welcome Prof <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/anthony-brookes/" data-type="people" data-id="18615">Anthony Brookes</a> from the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/university-of-leicester/" data-type="member" data-id="18580">University of Leicester</a> and Associate Director of the Midlands Substantive Site for the Health Data Research UK Institute (<a href="https://elixiruknode.org/members/hdr-uk/" data-type="member" data-id="18557">HDR UK</a>). He will be presenting federated discovery of datasets via meta.</p>

<p>Prof Anthony Brookes’ research and development activities are focused on human genome variation and disease with a core interest in knowledge management to bridge research and healthcare via data science tools and methods. Recent outputs include the GA4GH/IRDiRC standard ADA-M and Beacon, the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/gwas-central/" data-type="service" data-id="18722">GWAS Central database</a> and the <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/cafe-variome/" data-type="service" data-id="18702">Café Variome</a> discovery platform.</p>
