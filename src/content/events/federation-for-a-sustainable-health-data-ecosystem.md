<p>The HDR UK Federated Analytics programme is working with existing platform providers, TREs, and the public to drive the adoption of standards, build trust, enable federation, and accelerate research.</p>

<p>In this UK Human Data &amp; Tech Club community call, the team will highlight the role federation can play in sustaining health data research, demonstrate federated querying using&nbsp;<a href="https://docs.federated-analytics.ac.uk/five_safes_tes" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">Five Safes TES</a>, and identify ongoing challenges and next steps.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="speaker">Speaker</h3>

<p><strong>Jonaa Eva</strong><br>Project and Outreach Senior Manager<br><em>University of Nottingham / HDR UK</em></p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h3>

<p>A 30-20-minute presentation followed by a 10-minute Q&amp;A. This session will be recorded.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-join">Who should join</h3>

<p>This session is particularly relevant for:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>People building tools, platforms, frameworks, or infrastructure to enable federation</li>

<li>People operating TREs, data platforms, or secure research infrastructures</li>

<li>People coordinating governance, policy, and standards for federation</li>

<li>People funding, supporting, or commissioning federated research</li>

<li>People conducting research that relies on cross-TRE data access</li>
</ul>
