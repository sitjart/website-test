<p class="has-medium-font-size">Two workshops. One community. A growing impact.</p>

<p>The ELIXIR All Hands Meeting (AHM) is one of the most <strong>competitive spaces for a slot</strong> in the ELIXIR calendar of events – and this year, the leads of the Human Data &amp; Tech Club have earned not one but <strong>two workshops</strong> on the programme. Securing two slots is real evidence of the relevance of the work of our members in our UK community.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="whats-happening-in-lyon">What's happening in Lyon</h3>

<p><strong>Workshop 1 – Monday 9 June</strong><br><em>Bringing health data standards across Europe: From OMOP to GA4GH</em><br>Chaired by Wendi Bacon, Tim Beck, and Saskia Lawson-Tovey, this session brings together ELIXIR Nodes to align on health data standards including the OMOP Common Data Model and GA4GH standards such as Beacon, Phenopackets, and Passports/AAI. Featuring real-world use cases from HDR UK's Cohort Discovery tool and the 5 Safes TES, plus lightning talks from 14 ELIXIR Nodes and collaborative breakout discussions.</p>

<p><strong>Workshop 2 – Tuesday 10 June</strong><br><em>Connecting health data pan-Europe: how can we help each other?</em><br>Also chaired by Saskia Lawson-Tovey, Wendi Bacon, and Tim Beck, this session takes a community-building focus. With lightning talks from over 20 ELIXIR Nodes, it will surface shared challenges around GDPR, sensitive data infrastructure, and governance – and explore how Nodes can better support each other as the dedicated Health Data Focus Group winds down.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h3>

<p>Following the AHM, our HDT Club call will hear directly from those who led and participated in Lyon. We'll share:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Key takeaways and outcomes from both workshops</li>

<li>Highlights from the node lightning talks across Europe</li>

<li>Next steps for cross-node collaboration and community connection</li>

<li>Plans for the post-workshop reports</li>
</ul>

<p>Whether you were in Lyon or not, this is your chance to stay connected with the health data community's direction of travel across ELIXIR.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-is-this-for">Who is this for</h3>

<p>This community call is for anyone with an interest in health data research and infrastructure across ELIXIR and beyond. You'll find it especially relevant if you:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Are a member of the ELIXIR-UK Human Data &amp; Tech Club or curious about joining.</li>

<li>Work with health or clinical data in a research context.</li>

<li>Are interested in pan-European data standards such as OMOP, GA4GH Beacon, or Phenopackets.</li>

<li>Want to understand how different countries (ELIXIR Nodes) are navigating shared challenges around GDPR, sensitive data and governance.</li>

<li>Couldn't make it to Lyon and want to stay connected with what's shaping the health data community space.</li>
</ul>
