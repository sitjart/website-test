<p class="has-medium-font-size">How can we strengthen connections and amplify the collective voice of bioinformatics core facility leaders and aspiring leaders across the UK?</p>

<p>Join this ELIXIR-UK community session to explore the development of BioinfoCoreLeaders UK and the working groups that have emerged from it, and to find out how our online community supports ongoing exchange and collaboration. It follows two BioinfoCoreLeaders UK meetings held this year, and is the first online session of the series.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-to-expect">What to expect</h2>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Advocacy notes</strong> – notes from our previous meeting, presented for approval.</li>

<li><strong>A draft SOP</strong> – for responding to requests to review bioinformatics analyses conducted by non-experts using large language models (LLMs).</li>

<li><strong>A draft advocacy white paper</strong> – shared for further feedback.</li>

<li><strong>Working group updates</strong> – an update from each working group, followed by open discussion on shared experiences and the community’s future priorities.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-is-this-for">Who is this for</h2>

<p>Existing BioinfoCoreLeaders UK members, and colleagues interested in getting involved, including bioinformatics core facility leaders and aspiring leaders across the UK.</p>
