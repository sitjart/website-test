<p>The January Human Data Community call will kick off our 2026 programme of monthly meetings. This session will focus on <strong>planning, priorities, and strategic alignment</strong> for the year ahead, providing a space for open discussion and coordination across our UK community.</p>

<p>This planning and strategy session will focus on three core topics:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Partnering with HDR UK to form a joint community</strong>. Discussion on forming a joint human and health data technology community and exploring areas of mutual interest and collaboration.</li>

<li><strong>Proposal for an ELIXIR All Hands Meeting (AHM) workshop</strong>. Initial ideas for a community-led workshop, including scope, objectives, and potential contributions.</li>

<li><strong>ELIXIR Europe 2027–2028 funding scheme – Expressions of Interest</strong>. Early discussion of the upcoming funding scheme of ELIXIR for the Human Data and Translational Research priority area, with a focus on community coordination and shaping potential Expressions of Interest.</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="who-should-attend">Who should attend?</h3>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Members of the UK Human Data Community</li>

<li>Contributors to human and health data technologies, standards and infrastructure who are interested in joining the community activities</li>

<li>Anyone interested in shaping the community’s direction and priorities for 2026</li>
</ul>
