<p><a href="https://biofair.uk/"><strong>BioFAIR</strong></a><strong> is a proposed new UKRI investment of £34M</strong> to develop a federated digital research infrastructure (RI) connecting existing life sciences RIs, which will be assembled mainly through integrating existing services, resources and knowledge from across the UK. </p>

<p>With the project set to commence in the summer of 2024, we invite you to join the RDM Club with <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/people/gerry-reilly/" data-type="people" data-id="22924">Gerry Reilly</a>, Interim Director of BioFAIR.</p>

<p>The four core capabilities will be a data commons, method commons, knowledge centre, and community centre with pathfinder projects to validate the capabilities. <strong>This infrastructure will support the broad life sciences research community across the UK. </strong></p>
