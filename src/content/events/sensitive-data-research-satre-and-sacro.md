<p>The 2026 July UK Human Data and Tech Club call looks at two <strong>DARE UK </strong>projects under the <strong>TREvolution</strong> programme. The call&nbsp; will introduce SATRE and SACRO – the work to standardise how Trusted Research Environments (<strong>TREs</strong>) are built and to make the checking of research outputs safer and faster. This is timely for the group: ELIXIR recently ran a session on standards across Europe at its All Hands meeting, where many countries reported not yet using <strong>SATRE</strong> or <strong>SACRO</strong>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="what-this-call-covers"><strong>What this call covers</strong></h2>

<p>TREs are secure environments for research on sensitive or personal data, giving researchers access while minimising the risk of data exposure.</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>SATRE (Standardised Architecture for Trusted Research Environments) is the UK's first open, community-led specification for building, operating and evaluating TREs.</li>

<li>SACRO (Semi-Automated Checking of Research Outputs) tackles a different part of the same problem – the final, often manual, step of checking that what leaves a TRE carries no disclosure risk.&nbsp;</li>
</ul>

<p>The talk sets out what each does, where they sit within the Five Safes framework, and what adoption has looked like so far.</p>
