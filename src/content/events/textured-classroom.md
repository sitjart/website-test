<p class="has-medium-font-size">Exploring when and how friction and smoothness should be used in teaching and learning</p>

<p>This Training Club session explores <strong>friction in learning</strong>. Friction is an obstacle that requires effort to overcome – it can cause discomfort. But when used well, it can be a powerful tool for deepening understanding.</p>

<p>Through a&nbsp;<strong>short presentation and discussions</strong>, we will interrogate when and how friction and smoothness should be used in teaching and learning.</p>

<p><strong>The session will cover:</strong></p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>The difference between friction and smoothness</li>

<li>Interrogating whether friction should be used</li>

<li>Discussions on jargon, coding and dyslexia</li>
</ul>
