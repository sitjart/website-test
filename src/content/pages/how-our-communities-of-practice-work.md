<p>ELIXIR uses several terms for its groups – clubs, Platforms, Communities and Focus Groups – but <strong>we see them all as communities of practice</strong>: people who share an interest and goal and work towards it by learning from each other and collaborating together.</p>

<p>ELIXIR-UK members can join our UK clubs or ELIXIR’s Europe-wide groups. Here’s how they differ and how to get started.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="find-the-right-group"><strong>Find the right group</strong></h2>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>UK clubs</strong> – our UK-based groups, open to anyone. Everything else below is Europe-wide.</li>

<li><strong>Platforms</strong> – overarching areas, such as data, tools or training, that span the work of many Communities.</li>

<li><strong>Communities</strong> – established groups focused on a specific life science domain.</li>

<li><strong>Focus Groups</strong> – newer groups working on a specific topic for a set period, after which they either become a Community or close.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="before-you-join"><strong>Before you join</strong></h2>

<p>The more you contribute, the more you get out of any group – but most contribution is in-kind, so expect to give a significant amount of your own time before you see the benefits.</p>

<p>Active club members are well placed for our internal funding <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/opportunities-past/" data-type="page" data-id="24411">opportunities</a>, which offer flexible funds to attend conferences, run workshops or support your services. To apply, you need to work at one of our <a href="/members">member organisations</a>.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="new-to-elixir-communities"><strong>New to ELIXIR communities?</strong></h2>

<p>Anyone can join any of the communities of practice at all levels, but understanding how ELIXIR’s Europe-wide ones work can be difficult at first. <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/contact-us/" data-type="page" data-id="19891">Contact</a> us and we’ll pair you with a UK member already involved in the group you’re interested in.</p>
