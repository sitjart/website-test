<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="benefits-of-becoming-an-elixir-uk-service">Benefits of becoming an ELIXIR-UK service</h2>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Influence the development of a major effort in data integration: standards, implementations, tools</li>

<li>Exposure to a wide range of potential users and collaborators across Europe and globally</li>

<li>Although Node Service status does not confer any additional funding, it does indicate the value placed on the service by ELIXIR, which may be useful for future funding</li>

<li>Those offering Node Services are eligible to apply for Hub funding for implementation studies and can also apply for Node funding for participation in ELIXIR</li>

<li>The most significant benefit is the raised profile throughout ELIXIR, its communities and platforms.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="selection-criteria-and-process">Selection criteria and process</h2>

<p>The selection panel assesses services based on:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Alignment with the five ELIXIR infrastructure themes (data, tools, compute, interoperability, training)</li>

<li>Impact</li>

<li>Level of usage</li>

<li>Sustainability</li>
</ul>

<p>The process for selecting ELIXIR-UK Services, including selection criteria, is open and transparent, published, and peer-reviewed.</p>

<p>Hancock JM, Game A, Ponting CP and Goble CA. An open and transparent process to select ELIXIR Node Services as implemented by ELIXIR-UK [version 2; peer review: 2 approved, 1 approved with reservations].&nbsp;<em>F1000Research</em>&nbsp;2017,&nbsp;<strong>5</strong>(ELIXIR):2894 (<a href="https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.10473.2" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener">https://doi.org/10.12688/f1000research.10473.2</a>)</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading" id="prerequisites">Prerequisites</h2>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Please note that for a Service to be considered, it must be associated with an ELIXIR-UK Member Organisation.&nbsp;<a href="/members">See full list of Members</a></li>

<li>All Services are associated with one or more of the&nbsp;<a href="https://elixir-europe.org/platforms">&nbsp;ELIXIR Platforms</a>&nbsp;(Training, Compute, Data, Interoperability, Tools) and typically serve one or more of the&nbsp;<a href="https://elixir-europe.org/communities">ELIXIR Communities</a>.</li>
</ul>
