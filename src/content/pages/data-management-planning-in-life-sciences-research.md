<h2 id="gspb_heading-id-gsbp-7b1ca68d-b5f8" class="gspb_heading gspb_heading-id-gsbp-7b1ca68d-b5f8 ">Why is data management planning important?</h2>

<div id="gspb_text-id-gsbp-4e99cb98-015f" class="gspb_text gspb_text-id-gsbp-4e99cb98-015f "><strong>Data Management Plans</strong> (DMPs) are a required part of funding applications to UKRI and other major funders. They outline how data will be collected, documented, shared, stored, and preserved — aligning with the <strong>FAIR principles</strong>: <strong>Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable</strong>.</div>

<div id="gspb_text-id-gsbp-d274c1f" class="gspb_text gspb_text-id-gsbp-d274c1f ">Yet DMPs often fall into a “set and forget” mindset. ELIXIR-UK and the wider ELIXIR consortium are working to change this by promoting DMPs as <strong>living, machine-actionable documents</strong> that support the project or data throughout its lifecycle.</div>
</div>

<h2 id="gspb_heading-id-gsbp-b36edaec-c188" class="gspb_heading gspb_heading-id-gsbp-b36edaec-c188 " data-aos="flip-up" data-aos-delay="100" data-aos-easing="ease" data-aos-duration="700">Custom DMP templates using the Data Stewardship Wizard</h2>

<p>Through work supported by the UKRI-funded <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/collaborator/biofair/" data-type="collaborator" data-id="22923">BioFAIR</a> digital research infrastructure, ELIXIR-UK is developing and promoting UK-specific templates in the <a href="https://ds-wizard.org/" data-type="link" data-id="https://ds-wizard.org/">Data Stewardship Wizard (DSW)</a> – an ELIXIR-recommended interoperability resource and a <strong>Horizon Europe-recommended resource </strong>for DMP creation.</p>

<p>These templates reflect the requirements of UK funders, such as <strong>BBSRC</strong> and <strong>MRC</strong>, and allow institutions to:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Adopt headings of common DMP templates from funders&nbsp;</li>

<li>Add sections for specific data types or policies</li>

<li>Provide reusable boilerplate text to ensure consistent wording</li>

<li>Link their DMPs to community standards, databases and policies via DSW's link to <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/service/fairsharing/" data-type="service" data-id="18720">FAIRsharing</a>.</li>
</ul>
