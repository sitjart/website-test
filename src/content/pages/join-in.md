<h3 class="wp-block-heading has-palette-color-4-color has-text-color has-link-color has-medium-font-size" id="be-part-of-elixir-uk-s-vibrant-community">Be part of ELIXIR-UK’s vibrant community</h3>

<p>There are many ways to connect with us and get involved. We welcome participants from across the UK’s diverse life sciences landscape – whether you’re a researcher, data steward, trainer, or infrastructure developer.</p>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="join-our-uk-based-communities">Join our UK-based communities</h3>

<p>By joining a club, you’ll find people who face the same issues you do – and who are willing to share what works. Whether you’re just getting started or already driving change in your organisation, there’s a place for you here.</p>

<p>Each group meets regularly online and at national events. These are spaces for informal exchange, co-creation, and lasting connections – not just mailing lists.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="gain-access-to-the-elixir-europe-communities-or-focus-groups">Gain access to the ELIXIR Europe Communities or Focus Groups</h3>

<p><strong>The easiest way in is through us.</strong><br>ELIXIR’s Communities and Focus Groups connect researchers across Europe around shared goals – from human to training and metadata standards.</p>

<p>If you're interested in joining one:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Start by exploring our website</strong> to see which <a href="https://elixiruknode.org/activities/" data-type="type-of-activity" data-id="79" target="_blank" rel="noreferrer noopener nofollow">Communities </a>ELIXIR-UK is most active in.</li>

<li><strong>Talk to one of our involved members</strong> – they can help you understand how to contribute and where your work fits in. You can find this information in the Communities pages.</li>

<li><strong>If your interest lies in an area where we have less presence</strong>, reach out to the ELIXIR-UK Node Coordination Office. We’ll support you in making connections and establishing a UK voice in that space.</li>
</ul>

<p>You can also browse the full list of ELIXIR Communities and Focus Groups on the <a>ELIXIR Europe website</a>. Contact details are usually listed at the bottom of each page.</p>

<hr class="wp-block-separator has-alpha-channel-opacity"/>

<h3 class="wp-block-heading" id="become-a-member-organisation">Become a member organisation</h3>

<p>Check whether your institution is already an <a href="/members">ELIXIR-UK member</a>. You can still join our communities without institutional membership, but only members can:</p>

<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li>Access funding opportunities at the ELIXIR Europe level</li>

<li>Access funding opportunities from ELIXIR-UK</li>

<li>Apply to become an ELIXIR-endorsed service</li>

<li>Contribute to national governance</li>
</ul>
